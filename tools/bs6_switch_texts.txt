Hand fixes of the Bootstrap 6 switch found by their exact text, read by
bs6_switch_texts() in tools/bs6_switch.php and applied by
`php tools/bs6_migrate.php --switch` after every scripted fix, so each
"from" is the text as the earlier fixes leave it. Every occurrence of a
"from" is replaced; a "from" that is gone while its "to" is not there either
is reported as missing (the text changed on development).
