  • Today the same "Değer ekle…" paragraph stands under both the subject and the text, each under its own "+ Değer ekle" button, and "Neden" carries a seven-line paragraph in a narrow column.
  • One way to write a value the subject takes chips while the text shows the mail's own word typed as ^^name^^. The words the e-mail fills per recipient join the list as the group "Alıcı" and are drawn as chips; a word typed by hand still works and is drawn as its chip.
  • "Add value" a 24 px button at the field's bottom right; its list opens under the field and says in its first line what a value is. No hint under the field.
  • Help one line under a field at most; a "?" at the right of the field's name opens the long text in a small window over the panel, Esc closes it.
  • Sender a folded row that shows what it holds ("Organizasyonum") while closed.
  • Every box's panel follows the same three rules, not only the e-mail's.
