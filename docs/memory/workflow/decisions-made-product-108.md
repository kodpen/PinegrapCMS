## What keeps the designer simple and quick?
- His second sentence is a rule of its own for everything added to the designer: `designer/designer-light-and-simple.md`. No new button that stays in a bar; one menu row, a default that needs no choice, further choices one level down; a part loaded on first use; nothing running while off; measured before and after.
- It binds these ten plans, the seven of part 106 and the steps not yet started of the four of part 105.

## What is the architect's reading, not his words?
- "Seviyeli" is read two ways at once. The order of work: the levels carry plan priority 7, 6 and 5, so level 1 runs beside the seven of part 106 and a lower level takes the places a higher one leaves. Depth in the interface: a feature is one row, its further choices a level down.
- With the designer plans before them, level 3 is likely to fall after 1 January 2027. The architect told him so the same day; which level must be in before the launch is his to say.
- No table or column is planned. The Activity tab is read from rows that exist; a store for a shared component's or a theme's changes would be a question to him.
- The ten were checked in the designer's source and memory first. Named snapshots, the command palette, Copy style, Lock, Outline and History's Compare of markup exist; the plans build on them and repeat none.
