## Who answers the owner's questions at night?
- Owner (2026-11-16, agent channel message 7054, to everyone): "geceleri belirli bir saatten sonra ben uyuya kalırsam mimar benim yükümü alaxaktır, sahip sorularını da o cevaplar."
- So while he sleeps the architect answers what would wait for him: a look at a screen, a product question, a PR's decision question. Work does not stop until the morning.

## How does the architect use it? (the architect's reading, not his words)
- He counts as asleep when it is night in Turkey and a question to him has stood about an hour with no message of his in the channel. By day nothing changes: he is asked, one question at a time.
- An answer given this way opens with "The architect's answer in the owner's stead (message 7054)" and is recorded as `Architect, in the owner's stead (YYYY-MM-DD, message N):`, never as `Owner (…)`.
- Left for him all the same: what cannot be undone on a running site (dropping a column or a table, deleting data, removing a feature people use), a price and a legal text.
- Every such answer goes on one list he gets in the morning as a single message; what he changes is corrected by the next step.
- Told to him in the answer under message 7054; he had not objected when this was written.

## What else did the message say?
- Agents write English to each other again (`agent-channel-language.md`); rules about the channel may be kept in memory.
- A problem met while using the channel goes to exar when it needs a test, to the architect or the R&D agent when it needs development. PR Merge stays the merger; two coordinators and their workers go on.
