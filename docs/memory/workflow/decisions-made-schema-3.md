## Which schema was approved on 2026-10-29?
- Checkout page on the order (#2984, architect, 11.0.120, built in A3a #2998): `orders.checkout_page_id INT UNSIGNED NOT NULL DEFAULT 0` by `install_add_column()`, written where the page is known (the redirect and the completing UPDATE), read behind `pg_schema_has()`; the webhook completion takes the receipt settings and the auto-registration switch from it, 0 falls back to a plain-text receipt and no account (`commerce/order-completion-from-payment.md`). General labels continue from 11.0.120.
- Legal acceptance on the order (#3001, architect, 11.0.121, built in #3021): `orders.legal_acceptance TEXT NULL`, JSON `{types, ip, user_agent, at}` written at the redirect when the documents feature is on; the webhook completion hands them to `pg_legal_documents_store()`; NULL: preinfo and contract accepted, kvkk not, the order's IPv4, no browser. Reason: the evidence and the consent rows are the visitor's own; `orders.ip_address` holds IPv4 only.
- Collection item revisions C7 (#3051, architect, the next free general label when pushed): `record_revisions` by `install_create_table()` in the `page_revisions` shape (`revision_no` unique per item and taken in the INSERT, `values_json` MEDIUMTEXT, `record_json`, `values_hash`, `user_id`, `source` VARCHAR(16); no foreign keys, files by `file_id` only); collection forms only; panel edit, restore, API and Workspace write one, a `complete`-only change none; retention by the page settings, daily sweep; erasure deletes them, the export shows current values; restore under the edit screen's rights; no API endpoints in C7 (`plans/designer-collections-steps.md`).
- Cart quotes slice 5 (#3072, architect, the next free general label when pushed): `cart_quotes` (token hash and hint in the `ws_approval_guests` shape, `contact_id`, `valid_until`, `status` VARCHAR(16): open, ordered, expired, cancelled, `order_id`, `created_by`, timestamps) and `cart_quote_items` (`cart_quote_id`, `product_id`, `quantity`; no price column: catalogue prices when the link is used) by `install_create_table()`, both in `get_tables()`; `cart_quotes.contact_id` a `pg_contact_holders()` holder (merge move, erase find) and in the privacy export. A note column or a default validity may follow by `install_add_column()`.
- Saved replies slice 10 (#3075, architect, the next free Workspace label when pushed): `ws_saved_replies` (`user_id`, `scope` VARCHAR(16) NOT NULL DEFAULT 'personal', `shortcut` VARCHAR(32), `title` VARCHAR(100), `body` TEXT, timestamps; UNIQUE (`user_id`, `scope`, `shortcut`)) by `install_create_table()`; this slice reads `personal` only, the team slice adds its right column in the ladder files, no table change; rows go with their user; `{customer}` / `{order}` stay placeholders in `body`.
- Shipment labels step 3 (#3108, architect, the next free general label when pushed): `shipment_labels` by `install_create_table()`, in `get_tables()`: order, ship-to, `number` VARCHAR(40) (joins the shipment row on (`ship_to_id`, `number`) and survives its prune), `driver`, `reference`, `barcode`, `label_format`, `label_file`, `state` (active, cancelled), `is_return`, user and the created, cancelled and label-removed times; unique (`driver`, `reference`). Documents under `data/shipping_labels/`, served only by a panel endpoint with the order rights, removed by the daily sweep 30 days after delivery or cancel and 90 days after creation at the latest, erasure deletes the files and keeps the rows. The shipment row stays provider-neutral (#3073).
