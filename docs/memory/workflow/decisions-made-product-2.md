## What happens to a guest order the payment service's message completes? (#2984)
- Decided (architect, 2026-10-29; `commerce/order-completion-from-payment.md`): the checkout page's auto-registration switch holds whichever request completes the order. A3 links the order to the account whose e-mail is the billing e-mail (`decisions-made-access.md`, #67 item 8: no verification) and opens none; A3b (#3035, `commerce/order-completion-account.md`) opens the account without signing anyone in, and the customer's receipt copy carries the set-password link as on the visitor's path. A blocked address gets no account; the order still completes. Reason: the owner's decision (#2382) completes the order the visitor left, and the switch is the site's setting.
- The receipt is the one sent by the checkout page `orders.checkout_page_id` names (`decisions-made-schema-2.md`); an order that names no page (started before 2026.11.0's step 11.0.120) or whose page is gone gets a plain-text receipt with a fixed subject and the order number, always sent, and no account link.

## Who pays a PayTR instalment's difference? (#3057)
- Decided (architect, 2026-10-29; plan payment-driver-layer 4c2, done in #3070, `commerce/payment-driver-paytr.md`): PayTR instalments are offered by a driver setting (the most instalments, a `connect_apps` option, 0 or 1 = single payment as today). Who pays the difference is set in PayTR's own panel: the store, and PayTR charges the cart total (4c2a); or the customer, and PayTR's notification carries a `total_amount` above the started amount with `installment_count` ≥ 2, which the completion records as `orders.installment_charges` and `payment_installment` and adds to `orders.total` (not `orders.surcharge`, the card surcharge; #3095), as the checkout does from `pg_payment_checkout_totals()` for iyzico (4c2b). Any other amount still alerts and completes nothing. Reason: the product's rule since iyzico is that the customer pays the instalment difference and the order records it; what PayTR charged is what the order shows, whichever way the panel is set.

## Where does a page not on a visual design edit its layout file? (#3071)
- Decided (architect, 2026-10-29; plan `legacy-page-designer-retirement.md` L5a): a small layout-only editor screen for pages whose style is not a visual design (a classic style not yet converted, a `style_file` style, an older cell-based style): the code editor bundle on `pg_layout_read()` / `pg_layout_save()` through the designer's style-independent `layout_code` action, roles 0-1, read-only on a hosted site, as the designer's layout tab; `pg_layout_edit_url()` points the classic case at it, so L5 drops `get_layout` / `update_layout` with `page_designer.php`. Reason: the layout file does not depend on the style, so its editor must not wait for the style's conversion (#2437 keeps every site's editors while a classic style exists); `edit_custom_style.php` edits `style_code`, not the layout. A conversion hint may be added later.

## Which carrier integrations come, and in which order? (#3073)
- Owner (2026-10-29, "ilk önce açık entegrasyonu olanlarla başlayalım … sonunda hepsi olacak … yurt dışı için de seçenekler olacak"): the order is the architect's; start with the services whose integration is open (public reference and test mode); every aggregator and direct carrier follows in time; international shipping drivers come too, the US and Europe first, because the product is global. Architect: Geliver is the first label driver (`plans/shipping-carrier-labels.md` step 2); Basit Kargo and Kargonomi when their references open, Yurtiçi and Aras direct, then a global aggregator or carrier with a public API for US and Europe, chosen by the same rule.
