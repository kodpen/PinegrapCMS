## Does the E-mails screen get a button that reads the boxes now, and a mailbox's features? (owner, 2026-11-14, in the architect's window)
- The owner: "birde eposta için yenileme düğmesi koyalım. yeni epostaları kontrol eden ve indiren bir düğme. başladık artık kullanıcı başı eposta imzası kara liste vs. gibi gelen kutusu özelliklerini de ekleyelim."
- So: "Check e-mail" on the E-mails screen, a signature per person, a block list, and the next features of a mailbox. Plan: `plans/mail-inbox-features.md`.
- The architect's reading, under his "uyarlayıp iyi, işe yarar plana çevirmek sende": the signature is the person's and stands where the writer's name stands, the box's Signature stays under it. The block list belongs to a box or to every box, not to a person, because a box is shared; a blocked mail waits 30 days in a "Blocked" view. Recipient suggestions, Send later and the Sent folder are the "other features".

## Does a person get a calendar of their own beside e-mail, with private plans? (owner, 2026-11-14, in the architect's window)
- The owner: "birde epostalar takvim ile entegrasyonu da düşünelim (workspace daki takvim ancak kendi ekranı olur sadece kişinin takvimi gösterilir, etkinlik vs. varsa buraya düşer. takvim olduğu gibi workspace de kalabilir sadece bir kısayol koyarız eposta kısmına,"
- "kullanıcı plan ekleyebilir (planlar varsayılan diğer kişilere kapalı olur eğer izin verirse diğer personel yada kullanıcı görebilir.)) plan kişisel bir hatırlatma olabilir, yada yapılacak bir iş için yol haritası gibi olabilir."
- "ayrıca müşteri için yol haritası çıkarılırken bu takvim planları kullanılabilir iyi olur. ben kabaca anlattım uyarlayıp iyi, işe yarar plana çevirmek sende."
- So: the calendar stays the Workspace's. "My calendar" shows the reader alone and is reached from the e-mail area. A person adds plans; a plan is closed to everybody else until its owner opens it to chosen people or the team. A plan is a reminder or a roadmap with steps, and a customer's roadmap is built from plans. Plan: `plans/personal-calendar-plans.md`.
- The architect's reading: a plan is never a row of the team's plan items, because their readers do not ask whose row it is. "Closed" holds for an administrator too. An invitation in a mail and "Add to my calendar" are how e-mail meets the calendar.
- The tables are asked of the owner after the census (PC1).

## Does the customer see their roadmap on the site? (owner, 2026-11-14, in the architect's window)
- Asked: "Müşteri kendi yol haritasını sitede görebilsin mi?" The owner: "Şimdilik ekip içi".
- So: a roadmap made for a customer is seen by the team members its owner opens it to, on the customer's card and history. Nothing of it is drawn on the site or sent to the customer by the plan's steps.
- "Şimdilik" leaves it open: showing it to the customer is a later decision of his, not a step somebody adds.

## Does a chart of one series take its channel's colour? (owner, 2026-11-14, in the architect's window)
- Asked: "Tek serili grafikler kanalın rengini alsın mı?" The owner: "Evet, alsın". He had asked it of a coordinator first: "chartlarda birden fazla renk kullanılmayacaksa chart göstergeleri kanalın rengini alabilir mi?"
- So: an agent's chart of one series (bar, line, area, sparkline) is drawn in the colour of its channel; charts of several series and the donut keep the drawing guide's palette. A channel colour that does not read on the board gives way to its readable tone, then to the guide's first colour. Built by DG3 (#9467) and DG5 (#9469) of `plans/agent-drawing-guides.md`.
