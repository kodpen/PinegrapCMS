## Where are the rules and the earlier parts?
- `decisions-made.md`; the generated `DECISIONS.md` lists every part's entries.
- These twelve were asked by the R&D agent, one poll a question, each answered by the owner's own vote on 2026-11-16. The plans of the set (#9868) hold each answer beside the feature it binds.

## May a draft replace a published page, and may a change wait for approval? (owner, 2026-11-16, polls 63, 64)
- Poll 63: a draft may replace a published page outside an experiment too ("Evet, geçebilsin"), so several pages are published together. His decision of 6 October stays: a published page has no working copy and its own Publish goes live at once.
- Poll 64: a site may choose by a switch that a content editor's change to a published page waits for a designer's approval ("Evet, anahtarla seçilebilsin"). The switch starts off on every site; off, everything works as today. The plan's first step is a census.

## May the website feature plans add their tables? (owner, 2026-11-16, poll 65)
- Yes, their columns confirmed by the architect in each step's draft PR ("Evet, eklenebilir; sütunları Mimar onaylasın"): click map 1, content review 1, prototype comments 1 and a flag on the share link, visitor notifications 3, release bundle 2. A table beyond these is asked again.

## What happens to the rows of a record deleted for good? (owner, 2026-11-16, poll 66)
- They are deleted with it; the confirmation says how many rows go.

## How does the click and scroll map start on a new install? (owner, 2026-11-16, poll 67)
- Not decided. He wrote instead of choosing: "bu konuyu mimara danışırmısın. o tasarımcımız ve kullanıcı verisi işleme ile ilgili fikrini sunsun olgunlaşsın."
- The architect's view on the visitors' data is agent channel message 5774; the R&D agent brings it to him. No step of that plan starts before his answer.

## Who can be responsible for content, and who is told when a review date passes? (owner, 2026-11-16, polls 68, 69)
- Poll 68: any panel user who may edit that page or record; it works on a site without Workspace too.
- Poll 69: no bell notification to managers in the first version. An event is announced when the date passes and the site builds what follows with its own flow (a ready flow: a task to the responsible person).

## How does the share card start? (owner, 2026-11-16, poll 70)
- Off everywhere: it changes how a shared link looks, so the site's owner turns it on.

## What do customers see of each other's prototype comments, and how long are they kept? (owner, 2026-11-16, polls 71, 72)
- Poll 71: customers who open the same share link see each other's comments, with their names.
- Poll 72: a resolved comment is kept until the designer deletes it or the design is deleted.

## Is an app mode built, and what is the consent for news notifications? (owner, 2026-11-16, polls 73, 74)
- Poll 73: yes, behind its own switch that starts off. A site that turns it on installs to the home screen like an app, has an offline page, and notifications work on an iPhone. With the switch off the manifest stays "browser" (decision #5065).
- Poll 74: the browser's own permission question is enough; the visitor stops them on the site at any time; the yes and the stop are written to the consent log.
