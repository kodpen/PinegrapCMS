## Where are the rules and the earlier parts?
- `decisions-made.md`; the generated `DECISIONS.md` lists every part's entries. The part before: `decisions-made-workspace-47.md`.

## Is the Workspace's interface improved, its menu included? (owner, 2026-11-22, in the architect's window)
- Owner: "workspace arayüzde iyileştirme planlarmısın. menüsü dahil". He named no part and gave no picture.
- The architect looked at the screens on a sandbox with 13 channels in the two themes and at a phone's width, drew two sheets (`workspace/workspace-interface-drawing/`) and wrote `plans/workspace-interface.md`. The sheets were shown to him in the same window.

## What of his earlier decisions does the plan keep?
- The list's place (the main menu's Workspace panel only, no column or rail of the Workspace's own), its access rule and its links on other pages (2026-10-28, `decisions-made-workspace.md`).
- The list's order: search, Today, Channels, My discussions, Tools and More (2026-10-30 and 2026-11-03, `decisions-made-workspace-5.md`, `-6.md`); the bell as the one inbox.
- The channel screen as redrawn and its card look (`decisions-made-workspace-5.md`, `-13.md`); the message box's maximum width (`decisions-made-workspace-35.md`).

## What does the plan change of them? (the architect's proposals, not his words)
- The Tools: from tiles two across at the list's end to a dock that stays at the list's foot, cells four across, foldable to one row of icons; More from rows under the tiles to a card over the list.
- Channels not joined: from dimmed rows in the list to one row that opens them.
- A pinned channel's pin shows on pointing only; section names lose their capitals.
- The panel's foot stands at the window's bottom on every panel screen and is not printed on a channel.

## What does the plan add? (the architect's proposals, not his words)
- "All" and "Unread" over the list, "Mark all as read", a muted channel's mark, a draft's mark, the count of tasks due today or before on My Tasks (he called such a count a new request on 2026-11-11), Alt and the arrows to move between channels.
- One bar for every Workspace screen with the filters behind one button, one drawing of an empty screen.
- A "New" line over a channel's first unread message.

## When may its steps be given out?
- After the owner's word on the two sheets is written here. Until then the plan is a proposal: a coordinator gives none of its steps out, also where a step changes nothing he decided.
- What he refuses is taken out of the plan and the drawings in the same PR that records his word.
