## Bookings on the Workspace calendar and the Google/Outlook sync (#6302, architect, 2026-11-03)
- Decided (architect, BK6 of the appointment booking plan, `commerce/bookings-decisions.md`, PR #6305): no `ws_events` row and no schema; a booking's time has one source, the `bookings` row. One reader `pg_booking_calendar_items($user_id, $from, $until)` feeds the calendar sync (an item `ws-booking-<id>` beside the task items, derived id, dropped when cancelled, done or no-show), the read-back (never a move: written back as a refused move, the bell line names the booking) and the Work Calendar and Today screens (a second source drawn as kind `booking`). `pg_booking_agenda_ws_busy()` is unchanged. Reason: a `ws_events` row would keep two copies of the time in step on every path and need an ENUM widening and a column #5032 did not decide; the sync already works on derived ids without a table.

## Reward points of an order later cancelled or refunded (#6306, owner, 2026-11-03)
- Owner (2026-11-03, store credit points take-back, PR #6256, `commerce/store-credit-rules.md`): an order's points are taken back on cancellation and on a full or line refund; when the balance is short because the points were converted to store credit, the customer's newest `points` card is reduced or expired by the shortfall, never below zero. Not retroactive, no schema. Technical shape (architect): the amount is recomputed from the order's lines by the pure function that computed the grant; a product whose points changed in between is taken back at today's figure; the card change goes through the `pg_credit_*` path with a note naming the order. Reason: once points are money, the order → convert → cancel loop must close; the least new behaviour, no pending-points concept.

## Hand rows of a contact group filled by rules (#6319, architect, 2026-11-03)
- Decided (architect, contact groups filled by rules, `core/audiences-overview.md`, PR #6307): the recompute touches only `rule` rows, as #5033 said; it never removes or converts hand, form or product rows, re-reads `kind = 'dynamic'` before each page's write and ends without writing when the group went back to static. The merge keeps each row's `source`. The switch to dynamic shows the count of non-rule rows once and offers remove or turn into `rule` rows (default: touch nothing); its screen came in #6418. Reason: a narrow, empty or broken rule must not delete hand-added members silently and irreversibly; a removal happens only where the user sees and confirms it.

## Reset Encryption Key: warn and confirm now, re-seal everything later (#6392, owner, 2026-11-03)
- Owner (2026-11-03, #6392, option C): the reset keeps today's scope (card numbers and gateway secrets re-sealed, `ui/settings-security-cards.md`) but the card first lists, per area, the sealed values that will become unreadable (connected accounts and applications, marketplace connectors, e-document credentials, Paraşüt, backup storage, product feed tokens, the Google sign-in secret, two-step keys) and the save is refused with `mark_error()` until a separate "I understand" box is checked; one PR, the `bug` work item of #6392. Re-sealing every value with the new key in the same save followed as a plan, done in #6487, #6501 and #6506 (`platform/encryption-key-reseal-registry.md`). Reason: the silent loss stops at once; the full re-seal changes every sealing module and needs a failure plan for a value that does not open.
