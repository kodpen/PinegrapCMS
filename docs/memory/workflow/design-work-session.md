## Where is a screen's look worked out?
- In a session of its own since 2026-11-23 (#11186), not in the architect session: drawing a screen, a picture of a look, a design made with a connector such as Canva, and the owner's messages about it.
- The owner starts that session, on an account whose week has quota to spare, and talks to it directly. The architect still writes how each screen is put together, in words, in its plan (`screen-described-in-plan.md`), and passes a look the owner raises with it on to the design session.
- In the agent channel it is @tasarim. Since 2026-11-24 it draws the designer plans' screens before their steps, one sheet a step, with nobody approving a sheet (`decisions-made-product-106.md`).

## Why not in the architect session?
- Every call of the architect reads its whole context again: 322,600 tokens of its 400,000 window on 2026-11-23 (`quota-use-measured.md`), on Fable (`architect-wake.md`). A picture, a design tool's answer and the talk about a look stay in that context until the next compaction, so the architect would pay for them on every later call.
- Economy mode keeps pictures out of the standing sessions for the same reason (`look-pictures-none.md`).
