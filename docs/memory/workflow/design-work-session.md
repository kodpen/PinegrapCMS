## Where is a screen's look worked out?
- In a design session of its own while one runs (since 2026-11-23, #11186): drawing a screen, a picture of a look, a design made with a connector such as Canva, and the owner's messages about it. In the agent channel it is @tasarim; the owner starts it and talks to it directly.
- Since 2026-11-24 the designer plans' screens are drawn before their steps, one sheet a step, with nobody approving a sheet (`decisions-made-product-106.md`).
- Since 2026-11-25 no design session runs: it stopped for its account's quota (`coordinator-second-paused.md`). Owner (2026-11-25), asked who draws the waiting sheets: "sen çiz mimar ve tasarımcısın" (`decisions-made-product-110.md`). The architect draws them and settles its own readings of a plan.
- The architect still writes how each screen is put together, in words, in its plan (`screen-described-in-plan.md`); a part with no model on the Design examples screen it draws as before (`architect-draws-new-part.md`).

## In which order does the architect draw?
- The sheets of `plans/designer-drawings.md`, then of `plans/designer-drawings-levels.md`, then DD22 to DD26 of `plans/designer-options-census-steps-3.md`; a sheet that holds the most waiting steps comes first.
- One sheet, one docs-only PR, claimed as its plan says; a green one lands alone.

## How is the cost kept down?
- Every call of the architect reads its whole context again: 322,600 tokens of its 400,000 window on 2026-11-23 (`quota-use-measured.md`), on Fable (`architect-wake.md`). A picture stays in that context until the next compaction.
- So a sheet is written as HTML from a committed sheet as the model, rendered by a script, and looked at once per theme at device scale 1; no picture is opened again to answer a question, and none is sent to the owner unless he asks.
- Economy mode keeps pictures out of the other standing sessions as before (`look-pictures-none.md`).

## What happens when a design session runs again?
- The owner starts it on an account whose week has quota to spare. It takes the sheets no open PR claims; this file's first section is then corrected.
