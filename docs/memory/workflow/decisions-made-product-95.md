## How does the exported data of an information request reach the person? (owner, 2026-11-21, in the architect's window)
- "önerin yerinde", to the architect's proposal: a signed download link, valid 7 days, in the answer e-mail.
- Binds: the privacy centre's answer to an information request carries a link and no attachment (`core/privacy-request-export-link.md`).
- Why: an export holds personal data; an attachment stays in every mailbox it passes, a link ends by itself and can be withdrawn.
- Not chosen: the export attached to the e-mail; a download only after a sign-in to My Account (a person with no account could not take it).
