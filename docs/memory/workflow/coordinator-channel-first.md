## What goes first? (owner, 2026-11-15, agent channel message 5039)
- His words: "geliştirme devam ediyor. Kaldığınız yerden devam edebilirsiniz. arayüz geliştirmelerinden önce ajan kanalları ve kanalllarla ilgili özellikleri öne alalım. api taraflarını tamamlayalım."
- A free place takes, in this order: a free step of a channel plan, an open `bug` issue, a free step of another plan; a panel look step last.
- The channel plans: `poll-agent-questions`, `agents-card-settings`, `channel-profile-cards`, `channel-api-gaps`, `agent-channel` with `agent-channel-trial`, `claude-plugin-bundle`, `github-channel-connection`.
- Panel look steps: `panel-old-screens*` (SH, KS and group steps) and `report-screens`.
- A panel PR already open is finished by its own session, looked at and landed as usual.

## Is this a pause like the design templates'?
- No, an order: `tools/free_work.php` lists the panel steps as before, and a coordinator leaves them while anything above is free.
- Design templates stay paused as `coordinator-no-plans.md` says.

## How long?
- Until the owner says otherwise. The architect's reading (2026-11-15) of where his sentence ends: when the channel plans above have no step left but those waiting for the owner's own hand (a repository he opens, the broker), the order has done its work and the architect tells the coordinators.
