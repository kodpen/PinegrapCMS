## Which features of the world's products does the product get next? (owner, 2026-11-27, agent channel poll 246, message 14833)
- Owner (2026-11-27, agent channel message 14812): "sende geliştirme önerisiyle gel, türkiye değil global de en değerli 10 özelliği titizlikle tespit et (bugün pinegrap developmentda olmayan)".
- What was found: most of what the world's site, shop and team products offer is already built; what is missing gathers around selling outside Turkey. Ten were offered with their reasons (agent channel message 14832), several answers allowed. The order was the architect's judgement, not a measured demand; the outside figures were read from summaries of their sources.
- Owner: nine of the ten.
  - Local payment methods and pay-later (iDEAL, Bancontact, SEPA, BLIK, Klarna) through Stripe.
  - Panel language packs (German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Arabic) and a right-to-left panel.
  - Markets: a price list a country, and the charge in the buyer's currency.
  - Automatic tax: the EU's VAT rate by the buyer's country with an OSS report, and US sales tax.
  - Global sales channels: Amazon, eBay, Etsy, TikTok Shop.
  - A whole site from one brief, made by AI: pages, menu and theme.
  - A knowledge base and support requests.
  - QuickBooks and Xero sync.
  - Importers from Wix, Squarespace and Webflow.
- Not chosen: the European e-invoice (EN 16931, Peppol). It is not planned and not offered again unless he asks.
- So each of the nine gets a plan at priority 5, behind the designer's faults, the open plans and the Workspace's own screens; the list and their order are in `plans/global-features.md`.
- Architect's reading, not his words: his yes is to the feature, not to a table. A plan whose build needs a table or a column starts with a census step, and each table is asked of him on its own (Fixed rules 5).
