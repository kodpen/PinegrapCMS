## Which design template work runs during the pause?
- Owner (2026-11-11): the polishing round of the shipped templates (`plans/design-template-own-looks.md`) runs again as one small line on his own machine: a few local sessions, started and watched by that account's coordinator.
- They take `appstudio` (Mobile App Developer) and `dealership` (Car Dealership) first, ahead of [[design-template-own-looks-order]].
- Owner (2026-11-11): one more coordinator takes the rest of the round in the order of that file, with at most two workers on it at a time (his answer later that day). It is the coordinator that the coordinator of his own line told by message; no other coordinator reads this as leave.
- Why two lines: most of the round is still open, and the account of his own line had little of its weekly allowance left.
- Everybody else stays under the pause ([[coordinator-no-plans]]): no other session takes a template step, and no new template is started.

## What did the owner ask for?
- The shipped templates look like one type: each gets design changes of its own, bolder, with motion.
- The theme system stays: looks and palettes are kept, and new ones are added.
- He named Apple's iPhone page for the Mobile App Developer template and Tesla's Model 3 page for the car template, as examples of a design language only. Nothing of theirs is copied: no text, picture, name, logo or layout.

## How does a session of these lines work?
- By [[design-template-own-look]]: the template's palette block family first, then its home and signature pages; motion only from the interactions on nodes, never a template's own JavaScript.
- Its PR names the owner's order of 2026-11-11 in the body, so that it is not read as a breach of the pause, and lands as usual.
- What a page needs and the Visual Page Editor cannot do yet goes into the PR body under "Needs from the designer".
- `tools/free_work.php` keeps printing the low-priority list as paused: the steps are taken from the order file.

## Where do the designer features go?
- Into a feature plan of their own, written into `plans/` from an inspection of the Visual Page Editor. It is an ordinary feature plan, not a template plan: any coordinator's workers take its free steps.
- The first one, `designer-bold-pages` (twelve steps, #8685 to #8976), is finished and deleted. What it built is in the `designer/` files: full-screen media and giant type, scroll-linked effects, counting numbers, the Variant Picker, the Product Bar, wide slides, the Pinned Story, the product looks and palettes, the hero video, colour chapters, a design's colour mode, and template files without fixed colour classes.
