## Does a review stay verified and counted after its order is cancelled? (#7202)
- Owner (2026-11-05, option C, more than the recommended B): no. A review hides from the lists, the verified-only thread, `rating_avg` / `rating_count` and aggregateRating while its order is not bought (`pg_review_rating_sql()` and `pg_review_verified_filter_sql('yes')` add `EXISTS (… orders.id = comments.verified_order_id AND <pg_review_bought_sql()>)`; `process_order_cancellation()` and `pg_order_restore_cancelled()` call `pg_review_refresh_products()` for the order's products), and also when the reviewed item's whole ordered quantity on that order was returned and refunded (`order_returns.status = 'refunded'` joined through `order_return_items` on the order's lines of the product; the return refund path refreshes the products). A deleted order (row gone) counts as bought, so `delete_order()` wipes no history. The row stays and Comment Moderation lists it with its order; a restored order brings the review back. No schema; running sites hide such reviews at the next daily refresh. Two PRs: the waiting bug-hunt PR takes the cancellation part, the returns part is the `bug` work item #7202. Reason: Ticari Reklam Yönetmeliği 28/B publishes only reviews whose purchase stands; a cancelled or fully returned and refunded order is no purchase.

## What do cancel and refund do to purchased private folder access, and may such a product sit in a bundle? (#7204)
- Owner (2026-11-05, as recommended): 1B and 2A. Cancel (`process_order_cancellation()`, a full refund on the order screen cancels too) and a refunded return of the line take back what the order granted, without schema, as `recurring_payment_job.php` revokes by user and folder: limited access (`private_days` > 0) loses `private_days × quantity` days from the `aclfolder` row's expiration and a past date deletes the row; unlimited access deletes the row; `pg_order_restore_cancelled()` grants again through the completion function. A grant staff gave by hand on the same folder and user goes with it (rare; given again by hand); a product with only a send-to page has nothing to take back. Reason: a gift card the order issued is voided on cancel, the files it sold should not stay either. Refused: a table of grants per order (schema for a rare clash), a notice for staff (forgotten). 2A: `pg_bundle_component_problem()` refuses a component with `grant_private_access`, as gift card, recurring, donation and bundle components; a bundle that already holds one sells until its next save and the editor names it. Refused: granting per component at completion (opens the same question for membership, contact group, e-mail page and form settings of components). `bug` work item: #7204.
