## Does a gift card pay for a gift card product in the cart? (owner, 2026-11-17, agent channel poll 138, message 8802)
- He chose "Ödemesin: kart yalnız diğer ürünlere uygulanır": the applied gift cards and store credit together take off no more than the part of the total that is not gift card lines. A gift card product is paid by another payment method.
- Before, only an order of gift cards alone refused a card: one other line in the cart let a card, or a customer's store credit, pay for a new gift card (bug #10258). For store credit the rule was already his (#5039, `commerce/store-credit-rules.md`).
- The refusal of a card on an order of gift cards alone stays, with its text.
- Not chosen: "Bugünkü gibi kalsın: karışık sepette öder".
