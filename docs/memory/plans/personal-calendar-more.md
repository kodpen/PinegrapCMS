## What is asked?
- The owner ticked three additions to the closed personal calendar plan (`workspace/workspace-personal-calendar-rules.md`) and answered the export question of its PC6 (`workflow/decisions-made-workspace-29.md`, `-28.md`).

## Which steps?
- PC6b (S, after PC6) Done #9552 (`workspace/workspace-personal-plan-export.md`): a contact's data export lists the plans that name the contact and are shared with "The whole team": title, days, the owner's name, a roadmap's steps. A plan kept "Only me" or for chosen team members is never listed, whoever runs the export. A test holds the rule.
- PC10 (S, no product code, done #9550, files `workspace/personal-calendar-more-census-*.md`) Census: how a plan's days are stored and read (`workspace/workspace-personal-plans.md`), what a repeat needs, how a roadmap is copied today, what the task writer of `workspace/workspace-calendar-write.md` keeps per person. It names the column or table each step needs; the architect asks the owner before any is built.
- PC11 (M, after PC10) Repeating plans: every day, week, month or year, until a day or for a number of times. A change or a delete asks "This one" or "This and the following". The reminder rings for each time; My calendar, Today and the shared view draw each time by the plan's own gate. A roadmap does not repeat.
- PC12 (M, after PC10) Team roadmap templates: "Save as team template" on a roadmap keeps its steps with their days counted from the first; the team sees and uses a template, only its maker changes or deletes it. "New roadmap" on a customer's card offers the person's own plans and the team's templates. When a step's day changes, "Move the following steps too" shifts the rest by the same days.
- PC13 (M, after PC10) Plans written into the person's own Google or Outlook calendar, one way, by the writer tasks use: off until the person switches it on in My calendar, a private plan goes only to its owner's calendar, a change or delete follows, a plan shared with the person is never written.
- PC14 (S) Close-out: the memory files, this plan deleted.

- A template holds no customer and no person's name beyond its maker's.
- Look A (`ui/look-design-examples.md`), the whole picture set in the agent channel at once. Texts through `lang()`, Turkish by `tools/tr_add.php`.
- No table or column without asking the architect.
- PC6b beside the rest; PC10 first, then PC11, PC12 and PC13 in any order. Claim as `[plan:personal-calendar-more] <step> — <topic>`.
