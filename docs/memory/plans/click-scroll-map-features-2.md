## What switches it on?
- (C16) The switch "Clicks and scrolling" in Site Settings, Features, a settings text, off on every site, a new install too. Its card carries a sentence the site can copy into its privacy text: what is kept and what never is.
- (C17) Off is never hidden: the empty state of the Visitors section and of the designer's layer says in two lines what would be kept and what never is, and turns the switch on with one button for whoever may change Features.

## What does Visitors show?
- (C18) A section "Clicks and scrolling": for the period, the pages with counted views, the tenth half of their views reach, and their most clicked element with its share; a page opens its own view.
- (C19) A page's view: its elements (part, label, address, clicks, share of views), the share of views reaching each tenth, a device filter, "other" as one line.
- (C20) `GET /reports/page-clicks` gives the same figures for a period, a page and a device.

## What does the designer show?
- (C21) A "Clicks" toggle: badges on the canvas' links and buttons with their share over the last 30 days, found by the same four parts folded the same way as the script (a twin test); lines at the tenths with the share reaching each; a device choice.
- (C22) Elements counted on the site that the canvas does not hold are listed beside the canvas, not dropped.
- (C23) Only for whoever may open Visitors. An experiment's variants are not told apart.
