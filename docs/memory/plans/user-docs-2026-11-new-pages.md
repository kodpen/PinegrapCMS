## Why new pages?
- A subject gets its own page when it is a menu row or screen family of its own and would push an existing page past ~15,000 words, or when it is spread over pages today. Each new page has the same structure as the others (`workflow/user-docs-writing.md`) and links back from the pages that mention it.

## What does each new page hold?
- `/templates`: Choose a Template and Use This Template, live previews, then one short row per design template (about 55, News Portal to Zoo and Aquarium) with its pages and what it brings (booking, submissions, courses). Written from `designer/design-template-overview.md` and each template's file. `/visual-page-editor` keeps only how a template is started.
- `/marketplaces`: marketplace sync (Trendyol, n11 bulk matching, the ones before #500 moved from `/api`), product feeds (OpenAI feed, Google Merchant Center file and data source, product issues on System Status). `commerce/` marketplace files.
- `/flows`: Workspace Flows and Automation flows in the main menu: the canvas, every box by its label (task, For each, Pinegrap AI, MCP tool, Read rows of a table), publishing and running, following a run, quick setup, flows of members without a staff role and their approvals. `workspace/workspace-flows-overview.md`. The short Flows mention on `/workspace-cont` links here.
- `/workflow-tables`: typed columns, lookups and totals, the board view, sharing, filling from a spreadsheet or a note, ready-made tables, the query bar, the bin, the API and MCP. `workspace/workflow-tables.md` and the files it names.
- `/communication`: the Communication area and its tabs, incoming e-mail, order SMS and marketing SMS (Netgsm, İleti Merkezi, Twilio; İYS), WhatsApp, bounce and complaint handling, API mail transports, the sending-domain health card, double opt-in, visitor browser notifications, surveys once that plan lands. `ui/messages-area-overview.md`, `platform/whatsapp-overview.md`.
- `/privacy`: personal data export and erasure (KVKK, GDPR), consent log, double opt-in consent, checkout pre-information form and distance sales contract, cookie consent (moved or linked from `/site-settings`), what the audit trail records.

## What stays open?
- Whether bookings deserve their own page instead of `/calendars`: decided in UD14 by the page's length.
- The Turkish pages of the new addresses come from the owner's bulk translation run, never from a session.
