## When does a subject get a page of its own?
- When it is a screen family of its own and would push an existing page past ~15,000 words, or when it is spread over pages today. A new page has the structure of the others (`workflow/user-docs-writing.md`), sits in the Docs menu of every English page and is linked from the pages that mention it.
- A section a help button opens stays where its anchor is: moving it to a new page would send the button to the top of the old page. So a new page takes only subjects without a help row, or gets a short linking section on the old page.

## Which new pages were made on 2026-11-23?
- `/templates` (Design Templates, under Visual Page Editor): choosing a template and its look, the preview, the installer's site design step, Use This Template, what a template brings, then the 56 templates in twelve groups. Written from `designer/design-template-overview.md`. `/visual-page-editor#templates` keeps how a template is started and links here.
- `/visual-page-editor-cont` (under Visual Page Editor): more system widgets and layout starters, Video, conditions on widget elements, parts anywhere, address filters on record lists, form steps and payments, classic styles in the editor, drawings and App Studio blocks, prototype comments. `/visual-page-editor` links here from Editor, System Widgets and Publish.
- `/artifact-canvas` (under Files): opening the canvas, cropping and adjusting a photo, hiding details, selections, text and shapes, vector tools, layers, a blank canvas, animation, saving an animation, saving and versions. Written from `ui/image-editor-overview.md`. `/files#image-editor-artifact-canvas` stays as a short pointer.

## Where did the other planned pages go?
- `/marketplaces`, `/flows`, `/workflow-tables`, `/communication` and `/privacy` became sections of existing pages, where the help anchors already pointed; the list is in `user-docs-2026-11.md`.
- The Turkish pages of the new addresses come from the owner's bulk translation run, never from a session.
