- The strip never scrolls sideways and never wraps to a second line (D46): what does not fit makes room in the order below.

## In which order does the strip make room?
- One rule: the fullest drawing is tried first and the first step that fits is drawn. The strip looks at its own width, not at the screen's. The sheet's widths are samples: 1,130, 640, 380 and 150 px for screens of 1440, 1024, 768 and 390.
- Step 1: the chips lose their count; the name stays.
- Step 2: the open group's far tabs go behind the "+N" chip; the tabs nearest the open page stay.
- Step 3: the chips fold from the right; those that do not fit are listed in a "+N grup" chip.
- Step 4: the design's name becomes its icon, the name in the tip and at the head of its menu. The box loses the group's name and keeps its colour.
- Step 5: all chips become one chip ("4 grup").
- Step 6: only the open page's tab stays; a name that does not fit is cut with an ellipsis, the tab at least 60 px wide.

## What never goes?
- The open page's tab, its state (the changed dot, the draft pill, the lock) and its three-dot menu: at every step.
- The Pages button is outside the strip and stays at every width (Ctrl+P). On the sheet the dashed boxes are the top bar's other buttons.
- Architect (2026-11-17, agent channel message 8730): the Preview button beside Publish (DP23, #10244) is one of those buttons. With a saved draft (Save and Publish both shown) and a design name of 15 letters or more it stands whole on a 390 px screen; in DP23's picture 8717 it was cut off. DP18's look carries that picture.

## What do the chips open?
- "+N" lists the tabs that did not fit ("Sığmayan 4 sayfa"), each row with its state.
- "+N grup" lists the folded groups with colour, name and count; a click on a group lists its pages. A page opened from a chip opens its group in the strip.
- A design without groups has its tabs in the strip without a box; they go behind "+N" the same way (the lower half's first strip).

## What are the sizes?
- "+N" and "+N grup": 30 px high, corner 8, 11.5 px, the accent-soft ground with the accent line. A tab is 30 px high, corner 8 (D49).
- At step 4 the design's menu is its icon with the chevron; at step 6 the icon alone.
