## Marketplaces through an integrator?
- Entegra: "30+ Pazaryeri entegrasyonu", and 17 e-commerce platforms listed as supported (Ticimax, T-Soft, IdeaSoft, Platinmarket, OpenCart, WooCommerce …). Its API is given to solution partners on request; no public documents found.
- Dopigo: "45+ (artmaya devam ediyor) entegrasyon", public API documents (dopigo.readme.io). Sopyo: "+50 entegrasyon", a dealer and partner page, no API seen. Sentos and PraPazar: partner pages, no API seen. StockMount was not opened.
- ChannelEngine: "1,300+ channels", a developer hub and a list of technology partners.
- Being listed by an integrator is the same work seen from the other side: its customers can then pick this product as their shop.

## Channels through a feed?
- DataFeedWatch "2,000+ shopping channels and AI assistants"; Productsup shows 2,500+ channel integrations. They read a product feed, and the product serves one already (Site Settings › Ecommerce › Product Feeds).
- BirFatura: "+34 Ayrı Altyapı Desteği" and "33+ pazaryeri tek panelde", for orders and e-invoices.

## Carriers?
- In already: Geliver (7+ carriers by its page), Basit Kargo (6), Kargonomi, Shippo ("40+ global carriers"), Sendcloud ("170+ carriers"). Not in: EasyPost (100+ carriers by the reader's note), Navlungo, ShipEntegra.
- Geliver, Basit Kargo and the three global ones publish their API. Geliver keeps a page for each shop platform it supports.

## Payments?
- Craftgate: one integration for banks, payment and e-money firms, no count stated; ready modules for Shopify, Ticimax, WooCommerce and OpenCart; its developer portal showed a login page.
- Param: "23 banka entegrasyonu". Stripe: "125+ payment methods". iyzico and PayTR publish their documents and list the platforms they support; the product has drivers for both, and whether it is on their lists was not checked.

## What was not reached?
- The terms of every partner programme, and whether listing a new shop platform is free.
- The API documents of Param, Sipay, BirFatura and Netgsm; Entekom, Moka and the e-invoice integrators' own sites.
