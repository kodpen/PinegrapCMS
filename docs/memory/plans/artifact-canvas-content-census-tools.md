## How was it read?
- From the tools' public help, pricing and feature pages on 2026-11-26, no account. Canva's help refused an automated read (403): its points are a search's summary of the pages named. Numbers are the vendors' and change often.

## What does Canva give?
- Ready designs by purpose, searched, on any design type; Brand Templates locked by a team (canva.com/help/brand-templates-best-practices).
- Text: default text styles (heading, subheading, body) and font combinations dragged onto the page; a Brand Kit's own heading, subheading and body styles applied from the Edit panel, on every plan with a Brand Kit (canva.com/help/apply-brand-kit-text-styles).
- Elements: shapes, lines, frames (a photo cut to a shape), graphics, stickers, charts; the brand guide asks for SVG icons that take the kit's colours (brand-templates-best-practices).
- Photos: a stock library in the Photos tab; Pro has the large one.
- Brand Kit: colours, fonts, logos, text styles; switched from the Brand panel (canva.com/help/find-and-manage-brand-assets).
- Other sizes: Resize from the toolbar, suggested bundles or a custom size; Magic Resize / Magic Switch (Pro) makes the design in up to five new sizes at once, counted against the AI usage limit (canva.com/help/resize, canva.com/pro/magic-switch).

## What does Adobe Express give?
- Free: "345,000+ static and video templates", "1M+ royalty-free Adobe Stock photos, videos, music tracks, and design elements", "5,000+ fonts from the Adobe Fonts collection" (adobe.com/express/pricing).
- Premium: "730,000+" templates, "430M+" Adobe Stock assets, "30,000+" fonts, "Brand kits", "One-click Resize: Automatically resize assets for multiple channels" (same page).
- Text effects and AI text variations (helpx.adobe.com/express/web/add-effects-to-your-designs.html); templates with brand controls (helpx.adobe.com/express/web/create-with-templates.html).

## What does VistaCreate give?
- Starter (free): "100K+ free design templates", "1M+ Photos, Videos, Vectors", "Design Objects, Fonts, Music", "A Brand Kit" (colours, fonts, a logo) (create.vista.com/pro).
- Pro: "200K+ Design Templates", "170M+ Photos, Videos, Vectors", "Infinite Brand Kits", "One-click Resizer"; the resizer makes one design for several social sizes at once (create.vista.com/features/resize-design).

## What does Polotno Studio give?
- An editor built as an SDK others embed, so it is the nearest to what a CMS ships. Templates: "Start from prebuilt layouts instead of a blank page", searched and loaded in one click, for any page size (polotno.com/for-creators/features/templates).
- Photos "served from Unsplash through the Polotno API, with attribution"; "Icons, shapes, and illustrations come from the bundled element sets" (polotno.com/docs/side-panel-overview). Shapes, icons, arrows and stickers with fill, border, opacity and corners (polotno.com/for-creators/features/shapes-and-graphics).
- Text: several styles inside one text block (polotno.com/for-creators/features/text-editing); the pages read do not say what its Text panel offers to add.

## What do all four share at the empty board?
- A first screen of ready designs by purpose, not a blank page.
- Styled text ready to place: Canva's heading, subheading and body and its font combinations; elsewhere the templates' own typography (the other pages read name no text styles).
- Element sets beyond shapes: lines, frames for photos, illustrations, icons, stickers.
- A stock photo search inside the editor (all four; Polotno and Pinegrap's rich text editor through Unsplash).
- A brand's colours and fonts offered first in every colour and font field (three of four; Polotno leaves it to the host).
- One design remade in other sizes, a paid feature in Canva, Adobe Express and VistaCreate.
