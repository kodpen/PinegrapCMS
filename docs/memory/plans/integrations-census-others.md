## Where was it read?
- On the vendors' own pages on 2026-11-16 and 17. IdeaSoft, Shopier and PrestaShop Addons refused a read. The product the owner saw with "2000+" was not identified.

## What do the big figures count?
- Thousands and more are other people's work: WordPress "over 79,000 free plugins", Odoo "40k+ community apps", Shopify "over 16,000 apps", Slack "over 2,600 apps", BigCommerce "Over 1,200 apps".
- Around 2,000: HubSpot's home page, "Works with the tools you already use. 2,000+ integrations."; Zoho's marketplace, "2000+ ready-to-use extensions"; n8n, "Browse 2,000+ integrations"; Pabbly Connect, "2,000+ Integrations Available"; DataFeedWatch, "2,000+ shopping channels".
- The 1,000 band mixes a marketplace with connector tools: ClickUp's 1,000+ are "built by other companies", and Zoho Flow, Integrately and n8n are entries of its list. ikas shows a Zapier card: "ikas ile 3000+ fazla uygulamayı entegre edebilirsiniz".
- The figures are loosely kept on the vendors' own pages: monday.com 850+ and 200+, Calendly 150+ and 100+, T-Soft 300+, 200 and 228.

## What do the Turkish platforms state?
- Ticimax "100+ hazır entegrasyon"; T-Soft "300+ Uygulama", many of them storefront widgets; Platinmarket "130+ hazır entegrasyon", 68 of them XML suppliers; Faprika "119 entegrasyon ve eklenti", its own modules counted; Pazarix "100+ Entegrasyon"; ikas no total, about 50 apps shown.
- Their groups: marketplaces, cargo, virtual POS and payment firms, accounting and ERP, e-invoice, XML suppliers, SMS, marketing.

## Where does the product stand against that list?
- Has in some form: Trendyol, n11, PayTR, iyzico, PayPal, Stripe, Aras, UPS rates, Paraşüt, Logo İşbaşı, Netgsm, WhatsApp, Google Analytics, the Merchant feed; Yurtiçi, Sürat, MNG, PTT, HepsiJet and DHL through an aggregator's labels.
- Lacks: Hepsiburada (planned), Amazon, PttAVM, Çiçeksepeti, Pazarama, Idefix, Akakçe, Cimri; Sipay, Paratika, Moka, Tami, Lidio, the banks' virtual POS, Paycell, Papara, Param; Mikro, Dia, Netsis, Nebim, BizimHesap, Uyumsoft, Akınsoft, EDM.

## What follows for a claim?
- A figure above a few hundred is honest only as a "through" figure: through Zapier, through a marketplace integrator, through a feed tool, with the connector's own figure and its date.
- A native list is best given with its parts: so many marketplaces, payments, carriers.
