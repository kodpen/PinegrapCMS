## Which steps? (one PR each)
- K1 the embed: `PGWsFlow.embed(host, boot)` in workspace_flow.js. One live instance at a time (one `state`; a second embed destroys the first, whose host shows what it showed before). Stage and zoom buttons only, `state.edit` false, fit on load; boxes, arrows, meta lines, data rows and threads as on the canvas, threads always on; a `mark` overlay from the boot's dry run; `onSelect(node)`; `destroy()`. The wheel only while the host has focus (click, Tab), so the channel scrolls over a card nobody opened; pinch and the zoom buttons as on the canvas; the core's keys while focused, nothing of the editor's. Tests: tests/workspace_flows_canvas_test.php (its rows stay) and the harness, the same result before and after. Files: assets/js/workspace_flow.js and its bundle. About 500 lines.

- K2 the server side: `ws_flow_draft_card_view_read()` (flow_draft.php) gives the graph at its real places (never `ws_flow_card_graph()`'s scaled ones) with `ws_flow_card_tries()` per box, `mark` from `ws_flow_patch_marks()`, `flow_id`, `can_try`, `ws_flow_box_names()`, under the rights of `ws_flow_draft_card_graph_read()`; `ws_flow_embed_config($viewer)` (flows.php), the part of `ws_flow_js_config()` the drawing needs (kinds' labels, subjects, metrics, targets, repeats, events, tables, mcp names: labels only), for a home reader too, as panel action `ws_flow_embed_config`. Pure parts in tests/workspace_flow_draft_test.php. Files: includes/workspace/flow_draft.php, flows.php, actions.php, includes/panel/actions.php. About 300 lines.

- K3 the card: `flowWindow()` in workspace.js keeps today's picture as the resting state (the thumbnail: `ws_flow_card_graph()`, `flowWindowDraw()` without per-box tools); a click or Enter on it loads the two bundles and the embed config once, reads `ws_flow_draft_view` and calls `PGWsFlow.embed()` in a 20 rem pane ("Larger" doubles it); the strip below shows the picked box as today with "Open on the canvas" and "Try this box"; "Close", or a second card opening, brings the thumbnail back; after `sync()` an opened card reads the view again. CSS `.ws-fd-*` in backend.src.css; on a phone the pane is full width and pinch zooms. Tried on desktop and a phone (`workflow/sandbox-and-environment.md`), said in the PR. Files: assets/js/workspace.js and its bundle, assets/css/backend.src.css and its bundle. About 400 lines.

- K4 Edit here goes: `ws_flow_draft_box_edit()`, `_texts()`, `_fields()`, `_set()`, `_flow()`, `_read()`, `_save()`, the actions `ws_flow_draft_box` and `ws_flow_draft_box_save` (actions.php, panel/actions.php rows), `flowBoxForm()` and the graph's `edit` in workspace.js, their texts in `ws_flow_draft_js_strings()`, tests/workspace_flow_draft_edit_test.php; `workspace-flow-draft-card-window.md` loses its V7b section; `workflow/decisions-made.md` gets the owner's decision (2026-11-02: the card is read-only, the canvas is the one place a flow is changed; #3444's "Edit here" withdrawn: only a box's name could be changed there). First, so K3 never carries the form. Files: includes/workspace/flow_draft.php, actions.php, panel/actions.php, assets/js/workspace.js, tests, memory. Removes about 500 lines.

- K5 the other drawing: Add › Scheduled flow's mini window (`scheduledFlowWindow()`, `workspace-flows-scheduled-ways-in.md`) draws with `flowWindowDraw()` too and publishes its two-box flow there. If question 4 says so, its picture becomes the read-only embed of the sketch with Publish under it; else `flowWindowDraw()` keeps the thumbnail's duty only and the plan ends. Files: assets/js/workspace.js. About 200 lines.
