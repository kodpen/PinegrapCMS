## What is decided?
- Owner (2026-11-17, agent channel poll 129, message 8265): "Evet, böyle çıksın" on the sheet of discussion message 7612, `plans/designer-panels/phone-pages-move.jpg`. DP20 (Pages) and DP16 (Move) draw the phone from it.
- What the windows hold is the approved sheets' (`page-strip.jpg`, `overview-2-drag-move.jpg`). Only the sizes grow for touch, and what needs a keyboard or a pointer gets another way. Names and marks are samples.

## What is the sheet?
- Both windows are a sheet of the full height that opens from the bottom: it starts 58 px under the screen's top, below the top bar, with top corners of 20 px. Drawn in a frame of 390 by 708.
- Pulling the handle at its top (38 by 4) down closes it, and so does the × in its head (36 by 36).
- One layer is open at a time on a phone, as today.
- Opened from: Pages from the top bar's button; Move from the selected element's canvas toolbar and from an Overview row's menu. Ctrl+P and M keep working where there is a keyboard.

## What does the Pages window draw?
- Head: the icon, "Sayfalar" with the count, "Sayfa ekle" (36 px high), the ×. The search is 40 px high.
- The filters (All, Changed, Draft, Recently opened) are 32 px high, stay on one line and scroll sideways when they do not fit.
- A group's head is 34 px, with its colour and count; a folded group shows its chevron. A group longer than the list shows ends with a row ("12 sayfa daha").
- A row is 44 px: icon, name, address, state, then the three-dot button (36 by 44). The open page's row has the accent-soft ground and "· açık" after its address; a changed page has the dot before its icon, a draft its pill.
- A locked page says who has it open in the row itself, with the lock, in the warning colour ("…'da açık"): a phone has no tip.
- The keyboard hints (Up, Down, Enter, F2, Ctrl P) are not drawn.
- What F2 and dragging do is in the row's three-dot menu: "Adını değiştir" and "Gruba taşı…", each 44 px high.

## What does the Move window draw?
- Head: the move icon, the question on up to two lines ("… nereye taşınsın?"), the ×.
- It opens with the search focused and the keyboard up.
- A result row is 54 px: an icon tile of 32, the name, under it the path with the match marked. The chosen target has the accent-soft ground and a check.
- The place choice (Before, Into, After: a group 44 px high, segments of 36) and the "Taşı" button (44 px) stay fixed above the keyboard.
