## Where does a chosen box's side panel go (FW3)?
- The kind's title → the head: the box's tile, its kind (small caps), its title (its name on the canvas), then ⋯ and Close.
- "Remove the box" → ⋯ › Remove. Duplicate (drawn) → ⋯ › Duplicate: the box and its settings, no arrows, placed right of it where there is room, selected; one undo step; a start box has none.
- Close (drawn): nothing selected, the focus back on the box on the canvas.
- Settings tab: Title first, then the kind's own fields in today's order (retry, Tell me, the web address…); "Add a value" stays under the field it serves.
- Connections tab, its count the arrows in and out: "Comes after" → "Before", each box a row with its tile; "Then" → "After", each arrow a row: tile, name, its outcome select (a box with more than one outcome), ×. "Connect to…" with Add → "Add an arrow": the same select and button under the list; C still opens it.
- Try tab (the owner only, as today): the run to read values from, "Try this box", the answer; nothing moves.
- The foot line (drawn): "Before <box>" and "After N boxes"; each name a button that selects that box; several after: the first in arrow order.
- Which tab opens: Settings, and the last one chosen while boxes are chosen one after another in the same flow; Enter on a canvas box focuses the Settings tab's first field.

## And an arrow or a data thread chosen?
- The same head with the arrow's or the thread's icon: "Arrow · <from> › <to>" or "Data thread"; no tabs. An arrow: its outcome, ⋯ › Remove; a thread: its line, ⋯ › Remove the thread. Delete keeps working on the canvas.

## What does the panel show with nothing chosen (FW4)?
- The flow's head (its tile, "Flow", the name) and: start, boxes and arrows, last run, publish state; Home channel (the flow's own setting, today's field and its help); the problems and warnings, each with "Go to the box"; the Keyboard list folded at the foot.
- The draft help ("Select a box to change it…") goes: the empty panel's rows say it.

## And while Flow runs is pressed, or a run is shown?
- The runs list and a run's panel take the right panel as today (`workspace/workspace-flows-run-view.md`); a box chosen meanwhile takes it over, and Close gives the runs back.

## What changes on the canvas (FW5)?
- An arrow that leaves by a named outcome carries its name on a pill at its middle (Done, Not reached…); an arrow on "always" or the only outcome carries none.
- A box with a problem carries a rose warning mark at its top right; its tooltip the problem's text.
- Zoom and Fit stay at the bottom left; `fit()` leaves room for the left panel (seen in the harness).

## What is never dropped?
- Autosave and its states, the 409 rule, the keys, the outline's forms, data threads and binding, the run view, the embed: `flow-window.md` "What stays?".
