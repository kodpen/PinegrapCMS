## Which widths at 240?
- With the panel filling the column: a section card 216, a row's inside 192; the label column 78, a gap of 10, the control 104. The sheet draws the panel with a border of its own, so its widths are 2 px less. Everything that is clicked keeps a target of 24 by 24.

## What changes, part by part?
- Identity: the name on one line, cut with an ellipsis; the tag badge is never cut. The path shows its last step after "… ›", the whole path in the tip. One icon button, the three dots: its menu opens with "Copy style" and "Paste style", then its own entries.
- Tabs carry no icon. A tab's badge sits on its top right corner (16 px, a 2 px ring of the holder's ground) and takes no width.
- A label wraps to two lines at most; the dot stays at the first line.
- The quick strip: three cards as wide as their value, the last taking the rest; no caption line (it is the tip and the accessible name); alignment as its icon alone; a value cut with an ellipsis.
- A closed section's name is never cut; its summary takes what is left and is cut; where nothing is left only a colour square and the count stay.
- The screen-size chooser in a section's head is one badge with a chevron that opens the five sizes.
- The spacing box keeps its drawing with side paddings of 27, not 34.
- Texts get shorter and keep their places: the filter reads "What I changed" (the sheet's "Değiştirdiklerim"), the search "Search or add…" ("Ara ya da ekle…").

## When is a row stacked?
- Stacked: the label on a line of its own, the control under it at the full 192.
- When its control is a group of four or more written segments, when the control does not fit 104, or when the label does not fit two lines. A group of up to four icons stays beside its label.
- The group keeps one line of segments: this is how "button groups stay on one line" of `measures-2-anatomy.jpg` reads at 240, where six segments beside a label would be 17 px each.
- An invalid field's line spans the row and starts under the label.
