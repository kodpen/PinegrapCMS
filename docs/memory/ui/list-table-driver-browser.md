## What does the browser part do?
- `assets/js/pg_list_table.js` (bundle `pg_list_table.min.js`) starts every `.pg-list-table[data-pg-list-table]`; `root.pgListTable` is the driver, `PGListTable.init(scope)` starts injected markup.
- Selection by record id (`PGListTable.Selection`, pure, Node test `tests/list_table_js_test.php`): click, Ctrl/Cmd toggles, Shift range from the anchor, arrows and Space, Ctrl+A the whole condition (`as_of` now) with Ctrl+click exceptions; the bar's "Tümünü seç" does the same, "Tümünü kaldır" while all is selected (`allSelected()`, Node test). Replacing 2+ selected rows offers "Önceki seçimi geri al" for 10 s, a button of its own: `is-empty` follows the count only, so × hides the count and bulk actions at once (they waited for the undo before #6466). A row's link, button or field keeps its click; double-click or Enter clicks `a[data-pg-open]`, else the first real link.
- The screen hears `pg-list-table:selection` on the root (`count`, resolver `payload`); its bulk buttons may go in `.pg-list-table-actions`.
- Rows load by an IntersectionObserver on the foot and "Daha fazla göster"; a later request wins. Sorting, the sort menu and filters: `list-table-driver-sort.md`. A list printed whole (`all`) searches in the browser (`pgListTurkishMatch()`) the text a person reads (`list-table-search-text.md`). Search box: `<input data-pg-list-search="list name">`; new words drop a whole-list selection (undo kept).
- Browser mode (`data-pg-list-mode="browser"`): Ctrl+A selects the shown rows by id (`Selection.selectIds()`, payload `{ids}`); Shift ranges, arrows and the empty line skip hidden rows.
- Texts: `pg_list_table_texts()` (`data-pg-list-texts`, `%n` the number). No `aria-selected` on rows (not allowed in role `table`): the bar's count is a live region.
- The bar always shows (`is-empty` hides its count and bulk buttons; its look: `ui/list-table-layout.md`): its right end holds the columns menu (show, hide, up, down, Reset; `.menu`, `data-bs-auto-close="outside"`) and the "Detaylar" switch. Preferences are `{order, hidden, details}` in localStorage `pg.list.<user id>.<list>` (`data-pg-list-user`) through the pure `columnsView()`/`columnsToggle()`/`columnsMove()`/`columnsReset()` (Node test): the first column leads and never hides, nor does a non-hideable one; a lost key is dropped, a new column follows its model neighbour. Storage that throws: the choice holds for the page.
- `applyColumns()` reorders the heading and every row's cells by `data-pg-col` and sets `hidden`; rows loaded later are built in that order.
- Thumbnails: an `img[data-pg-thumb="url"]` in a cell gets its `src` once in sight (observer root `.pg-list-table-scroll`), at most 6 loading at once; the url comes from `pg_image_panel_thumb_url()`. A cell's `img.lazy[data-src]` (products, contacts, ERP) is taken over the same way: jQuery Lazy watches the window and ran at page load only, so rows below and later batches kept `loading.gif` (#6466). A failed one gets `pgLazyFailedSrc`.
- Layout (`backend.src.css`): the root fills the window from its top (`--pg-list-table-top`, set on resize), rows scroll in `.pg-list-table-scroll` under a sticky `thead` (no `.table` 3rem top margin), the bar stays above; a hidden column hides its cells. Frame, one-line rows, sticky name and actions, the details panel, phone cards, the SEO column: `ui/list-table-layout.md`.
