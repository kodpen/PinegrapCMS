Translations · the screen redrawn

One screen, translations.php, in three tabs: the texts with their source and translation side by side, the updates that ran, the glossary. The engine, its fallback and “Translate on save” are read on one line and changed in one dialog; saving, approving and filtering happen in place, with no reload. Sample pages, texts, people and counts are examples: a small shop translated from Turkish to English. Add ?theme=dark to the address for the dark look, ?f=A (B to J) for one board.
