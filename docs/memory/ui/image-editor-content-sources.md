## Which source and licence has each part?
- Own content, no licence line: Site colours (read from the site's designs), text styles and font pairs, the Elements shapes and frames, the thirty ready designs, Resize. The faces are Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License, as the Text tool's.
- Tabler Icons: MIT, its `LICENSE` shipped beside the file (`image-editor-tabler.md`).
- Unsplash: the Unsplash License; the API guidelines' hotlink, download call and credit are kept, so its layer keeps the returned address, not a files row (`image-editor-unsplash.md`). Shown only with the site's key.
- Openverse: only CC0 and the Public Domain Mark, the record's credit kept on the layer (`image-editor-openverse.md`). Keyless, for every site.
- A ready design ships no photo: a photo's place is a "Your picture" box, so no photo licence enters the designs (`image-editor-starts-designs.md`).

## What was left out, and why?
- Illustration sets: none chosen. A set under CC0 (Open Peeps) may come, its licence read again before it ships; a set whose licence forbids redistribution in a product or a competing service is never shipped.
- AI-generated designs or pictures, video and music libraries: not proposed.
- Anything from Canva: its APIs read no layer and forbid export into another design tool (`api/canva-api-terms.md`). A design made in Canva is opened from the person's own .pptx export (`image-editor-design-file.md`).
- A logo: no known setting holds it; a person keeps it in Files and uses Picture from Files.

## What do the other tools give at the empty board? (public pages, 2026-11-26)
- Canva, Adobe Express, VistaCreate and Polotno Studio all open on ready designs by purpose, offer styled text and element sets beyond shapes (lines, photo frames, icons, illustrations) and a stock photo search inside the editor.
- Three of four offer a brand's colours and fonts first in every field (Polotno leaves it to the host); Canva, Adobe Express and VistaCreate make one design in other sizes as a paid feature (Magic Resize, One-click Resize, One-click Resizer).
- Their libraries are far larger (Adobe Express free: 345,000+ templates, 1M+ stock assets; Canva and VistaCreate similar); the canvas answers with a small own set that a site can trust on every licence, not with numbers.
