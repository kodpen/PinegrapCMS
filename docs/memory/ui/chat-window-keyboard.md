## How do the list rows open from the keyboard?
- `activatable(node, key, handler)` gives a `div.pg-chat-row` `role="button"`, `tabindex="0"`, a `data-pg-chat-key` and Enter/Space beside the click. Keys: `user:<id>`, `conversation:<id>`, `ai`; the tabs are `tab:online` / `tab:conversations`, header buttons `sound` and `close`.
- The rows had only a click listener: a keyboard user could open the window but not a conversation.
- `#pg-chat-root .pg-chat-row:focus-visible` in `chat.php` draws the ring on `--pg-focus-ring-*` (`panel-focus-ring.md`).

## Where does focus go?
- Opening the window focuses the active tab; the launcher carries `aria-expanded`.
- Escape inside the window closes an open emoji palette first, then the window. Closing (Escape, the close button) gives focus back to the launcher when it was inside the window; before, the panel hid under the focused button and focus fell to `<body>`.
- Back from a conversation or the AI Chat focuses that row once the list arrives, else the active tab.
- On a wide screen the window stays non-modal: Tab leaves it. On a phone (`isFullscreenLayout()`, 575.98px and below) it fills the screen and the page under it cannot scroll, so Tab past the last visible control (Shift+Tab before the first) wraps round inside the panel; before, Tab walked the hidden page beneath it.

## Why does the 15 s refresh not lose focus?
- `refreshLists()` used to call `renderListView(null)` on every tick, which emptied the panel to "Loading…" and rebuilt header and rows. Now a tick keeps the drawn list (`state.listBody`) and redraws only the rows, then focuses the control with the key that had focus. Only a first draw or a tab change rebuilds the header, and it restores the focused key too.

## How is an open conversation read and scrolled?
- `openConversationView()` makes `.pg-chat-body` `role=log`, `tabindex=0`, `aria-label` = `STR.messages` (`lang('Messages')` in `pg_chat_panel_strings()`), with `aria-live=off`. `appendMessages()` switches it to `polite` 1 s after the first fill, so the opened history is not read out but later messages are. `#pg-chat-root .pg-chat-body:focus-visible` draws an inset ring on `--pg-focus-ring-*`.
- Before, the box had no name, no role and no tabindex: Safari could not reach it, the arrow keys could not scroll earlier messages and new ones were silent (`tests/scroll_regions_tour_chat_test.php`).

## How is the .min twin made?
- `chat_backend.min.js` is not a `pg_js_bundles()` bundle; it is regenerated by hand: `npx terser@5.51.2 chat_backend.src.js --compress --mangle --format ascii_only=true --output chat_backend.min.js` (`important-files-and-js-twins.md`).
