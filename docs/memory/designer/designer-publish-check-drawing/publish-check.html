Publish check

The sheet of the plan “Publish check”: the Publish sheet's rows get a result, a row opens its findings in five groups, a finding is fixed, shown or ignored, and the weight of a page is measured against a budget. Boards P1 to P4 belong to step PC4, P5 to P7 to step PC5. The designer's own windows are dark in both pictures.

  • Three results. “Ready” in green, “n to look at” in amber, “n blocking” in rose. A page with both kinds says “n blocking”; the others are counted inside. The state pill and “Changed” stay as they are today.
  • The result is the row's disclosure. It is a button with a chevron: a press opens the page's findings under the row. The rest of the row still ticks the box.
  • Busy. A ticked row shows “Checking…” in the result's place and the foot says “Checking n pages…”. “Publish selected” waits until every ticked page has its result.
  • Not ticked, not checked. Ticking a page later checks it then. A result is kept until the page's tree changes, so opening the sheet again does not check an unchanged page.
  • Keyboard. Tab reaches the box, then the result; Enter or Space on the result opens and closes the findings; Escape closes the sheet as today.
  • No new button. Nothing is added to the page bar, the canvas bar or a panel's head: the check lives inside the Publish sheet. The sheet is drawn 620 px wide instead of 500.

  • Five groups, always listed. A group with findings shows their count; one without says “Nothing found”. One row is open at a time; opening another closes it.

  • Blocking is marked in rose. The finding has a rose mark and a rose line around it and comes first in its group; the page's other findings stay amber and are still listed.
  • The row. The box is off and empty, the name is dimmed, the result stays readable. “Publish anyway” sits after the result, quiet and small, as on a held row.
  • “Publish anyway”. It ticks the row and opens one required field under it: “Why is it published with a blocking finding? The reason goes to the Site Log.”, 500 characters. “Publish selected” is off while a ticked blocking page has no reason; unticking holds the page again.
  • A blocking finding is never ignored. It has no “Ignore on this page”: it is fixed, or the page goes out with “Publish anyway” and its reason.
  • Fixing unblocks. When the last blocking finding of a page is gone the row is an ordinary row again: its box can be ticked and “Publish anyway” leaves.
  • Keyboard. “Publish anyway” is reached by Tab after the result; Enter presses it and puts the cursor into the reason field.

  • “Fix”. One press changes the page's tree as an edit does: one undo step, saved by the same Publish. The finding turns into a result line in its place, “Fixed: …” with “Undo”, and the row's count drops at once.
  • What a fix may do. Only what needs no choice: write a picture's own width and height, remove an empty heading, drop a left-over value of a closed field. Anything that needs a choice is “Show me”.
  • “Show me”. It closes the sheet and selects the element as the path does, and opens the place where the change is made: the element's option, or the page's settings for a finding of the page. The line over the canvas is the one of board P2.
  • Both are quiet. “Fix” and “Show me” are small quiet buttons at the end of the finding; the solid button of the sheet stays “Publish selected”.
  • The result after a fix. The page is checked again only where the fix touched; a page that has nothing left turns “Ready”.
  • Keyboard. Enter on “Fix” fixes and moves the focus to “Undo”; Ctrl+Z undoes the fix as any edit while the sheet is open.

  • The reason. “Ignore on this page” opens one required field inside the finding: “Why is it ignored on this page? The reason is kept with the page.”, 500 characters. “Ignore” is off while it is empty; “Cancel” or Escape closes the field and leaves the finding.
  • Ignored findings stay visible. They are not counted in the result and fold under “Ignored on this page (n)” at the end of their group, each with its reason and “Stop ignoring”.
  • This page only. The same finding on another page is still listed there. A finding that changes (another element, another value) is a new finding.
  • Never for a blocking finding. See board P3.
  • Saved with the draft. The ignored list travels with the page's draft: it is saved by the same Publish or by “Save this page as a draft”, and “Undo” takes it back like an edit.
  • Keyboard. Enter on “Ignore on this page” opens the field with the cursor in it; Enter in the field ignores; Escape closes only the field, not the sheet.

  • Four measures. Markup, Styles, Scripts, Pictures of the first screen: each a bar, the bytes measured and the budget, “262 KB of 200 KB”. The numbers of the budget are always shown, never only a colour.
  • Green, amber, red. The colour is doubled by the bar's length past the mark and by the numbers. A measure over its budget counts as one finding “to look at” (here two of the five); weight never blocks.
  • The weight findings. A picture more than twice its drawn size, a picture without width and height (“Fix”), scripts that hold the first paint.
  • Rules no page uses. One folded list for the whole design, the same on every page's row: the rule and its stylesheet. It has no button that removes anything.
