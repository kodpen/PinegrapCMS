## Where is the drawing?
- `designer/designer-options-states-drawing/`: `options-states.html` with a `-dark.jpg` and a `-light.jpg` picture; `drawing.css` sits on top of `ui/look-sheet/sheet.css`. The designer's own parts are dark in both pictures; the canvas shows a site's page in its own theme.
- The sample is the button "See the timetable" in the hero of the page "Home", design "Still Water Yoga". Values and counts are examples; the labels are the steps'.
- The State row (boards S1 to S3): `designer-options-states-drawing-state.md`. Transition, Transform and "More" (T1, R1, R2, Z1): `designer-options-states-drawing-motion.md`.

## Which board belongs to which step?
- DO10: S1 the State row in the Style tab (Normal, a state chosen, the first use, an element with two states, under 300 px); S2 a state's rows, "Reset this state", "Only what I changed"; S3 the canvas in each state.
- DO11: T1 "Transition" in "Shadow and effect" (nothing chosen, the list, the look's values, under a state).
- DO12: R1 "Transform" and the section's "More"; R2 the canvas while a Transform row is dragged.
- Z1 (DO10 and DO12): the State group, a head with its ring and Origin three times enlarged, the keys and what is read out.

## What does the drawing add to the designer's bars?
- Nothing: no button in the Options panel's head, the page bar or the canvas bar (`designer-light-and-simple.md`). One row first in the Style tab's body, and rows inside "Shadow and effect".

## Which parts are drawn from what exists, and which are new?
- As today: the identity row, the tabs with their count, the search, a section card with its count and summary, a row with its changed dot, a list, a field, a slider with its number, a switch, the info line, the first-use line with "Got it", "Only what I changed".
- New: the State group with a ring on a segment, the line under it with "Reset this state", the ring on a section's head, the summaries "same in every state" and "as Normal", Normal's value in grey, the mark "every state", the row "More" / "Less", Origin's nine places, the dashed outline on the canvas, the state in the selection's tag.

## Where does the drawing differ from the plan's words?
- The plan names a dot on a section that holds a state's value. The drawing makes it a ring, so it is not read as the changed dot, and puts it on the state's segment too.
- The plan's "one line saying why" is the summary "same in every state" on each dimmed card, not a line of its own.
- The drawing wins where the two differ (`workflow/architect-draws-new-part.md`); what neither says the worker asks of the architect.
