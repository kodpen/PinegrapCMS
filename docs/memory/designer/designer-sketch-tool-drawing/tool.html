  • The chip names the box’s kind. Its menu: Read from the sketch, ticked until a kind is chosen, then the thirteen kinds with their icons. The menu opens under the chip and scrolls inside the view where it does not fit.

  • New: a box without a pointer. With Sketch on and a sketch page selected, B adds a box as wide as the page, 96 units high, 16 under the lowest box (at the top of an empty page), and selects it. The same act is the first row of the page’s More menu, “Add a box”, and a row of the command palette.
  • New: size by the keyboard. On a selected box Alt with an arrow moves its right or bottom edge by 8 units, with Shift by 32; never under 24 by 24, never past the page’s width. The arrows alone move it, as built.
  • Tab reaches the pages and a page’s boxes as it does today; Esc on a box selects its page. Each new act is one step of the sketch’s undo. Letters keep starting a heading on a box: B is the key only while a page, not a box, is selected.
