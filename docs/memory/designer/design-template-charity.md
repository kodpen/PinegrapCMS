## What does it ship?
- Home (places `home_campaigns`, `home_events`, `home_posts`), `about`, `our_work` (a section per cause), `impact`, `transparency` (spending bars, `reports` list), `faq`; `donate` (gift amounts, bank transfer, pledge form) and `pledge_view`; the shop pages (`product`, `cart`, `checkout`, `order`, `email_order`); `campaigns`, `campaign`, `campaign_new`; `volunteer`, `volunteer_view`, `my_volunteering`, `staff_volunteers`, `privacy`, `email_reply`; `events`, `event_view`, `event_new`, `report_new`; the standard pages with the blog called News ([[design-templates-standard-pages]]); account and staff pages with `my_pledges` and `staff_pledges`.
