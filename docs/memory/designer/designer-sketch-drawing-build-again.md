## When does "Build again" ask?
- Only when a section would be removed, or a section edited in Design had its box changed. Otherwise the page is built again at once and the message says what changed.
- A section is matched to its box. One edited in Design is never rebuilt on its own: it stays, also when its box moved or changed size.

## What does the question show?
- The designer's confirm with its effects. Head "Build “Page 7” again?", under it "The sketch changed since the page was built."
- Rows, each a title and one line: "5 sections stay as they are" (their names), teal; "Features is built again" ("It has 4 columns now and was not edited."), blue; "Gallery is new" ("It is placed after Blog list."), blue; "Text is removed" ("Its box was deleted in the sketch; what was written in it goes with it."), rose.
- "More" is shown only with an edited section whose box changed. Open, it lists each: "**Intro** was edited in Design and its box changed in the sketch. It keeps your edits." with the tick box "Build Intro again from the sketch", off.
- Foot: the note "History keeps the page as it is now.", "Cancel" (quiet), "Build again" (filled). Enter builds, Escape cancels.

## What when there is nothing to build?
- A page with no box: "Build the page" is dimmed, its title says "Draw a box first"; the body keeps today's line "Drag here to draw a box".

## What when boxes are left out?
- The page is built without the boxes the reader skips: "**Page 7** is built: 7 sections. 2 boxes were left out: too small, or half outside the page." with "Show them" (quiet, small), which selects them in the sketch.

## What when the build fails or is refused?
- Failed: "**Page 7** could not be built. The sketch is kept as it is." with "Try again" (quiet, small).
- Refused: "You may not add pages to this design. The sketch is kept; someone who may can build it."
- Either changes nothing: no page, no half page; the sketch and the answers given stay.
- Each message stands under the frame it is about.

## What changes when the frame is small or the screen narrow?
- Zoomed out, where the frame is narrower than its name and the two actions, the chips are not drawn (as today under a readable size) and the head keeps the name and "More", whose first row is then "Build the page".
- On a phone the question takes the full width; its two buttons stay side by side under the note.
