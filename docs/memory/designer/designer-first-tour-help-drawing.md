## What is the Help window (T4)?
- The "?" button of the status bar opens one window, title "Help", 436 px, with two parts as a switch under its head: "How do I…" and "Keyboard shortcuts" (the list of today). The button opens it on "How do I…"; the ? key and F1 open it on "Keyboard shortcuts". No second button anywhere.
- Rows: "Take the tour" first, in the accent, a line, then the tasks, each 36 px with an icon and an arrow. A content editor sees only "Change a text or a picture" and "Publish".
- A task closes the window and shows the tour's ring and bubble on its first control, headed "How do I · 1 of 3", with "Close" where the tour has "Skip". A pointer shows where and never does the step; pressing the ringed control moves to the next. A control not on the screen is skipped.
- "Tips" at the foot of both parts: a switch with "One line the first time you open a feature", on at first. Off, no first-use line is shown; what was seen stays seen.
- Keys: the arrows move over the rows, Enter starts one, left and right change the part, Escape closes.

## Which tasks, and what does each point at?
- Add a section: "Blocks" in the palette, a block's row, the canvas. Sentences: "Open “Blocks” here." / "Drag a block onto the page, or click it." / "It lands where the line shows. Click its words to write your own."
- Change a text or a picture: the canvas, Options' "Content", the picture's field.
- Change the colours: "Design" beside Options, its colours, the canvas.
- Make it fit a phone: the canvas bar's screen sizes, the canvas, "Preview".
- Add a page: "Add Page", the new page's tab, the tab's menu.
- Show a list of records, Add a form: "System" in the palette, the row, Options.
- Use one part on every page: an Overview row's menu, "Shared" in the palette, the canvas.
- Go back to an earlier version: undo in the page bar, History in the canvas bar, a version's row.
- Publish: "Preview", "Publish", the page's tab.
- The step writes the other tasks' sentences in the same voice (a bold name, one short sentence) and maps each control to its id.

## What is a first-use tip (T5)?
- One line under a window's or a panel's head, across the width, on the accent's soft ground with a line under it: the sentence at 12.5 px and "Got it" at the right; in a narrow panel the sentence wraps.
- Shown the first time a feature is opened, for the features whose plan names a first-use line; each gives its sentence and its own name. The tour's part draws it, a feature only asks.
- It goes with "Got it" or once the feature is used, and never returns. Never two at once, never during the tour or a pointer, never over a dialog's own message. Read out once.

## What does an empty page offer (T6)?
- In the canvas's middle: "This page is empty. How do you want to start?" and up to three cards of 184 px (icon, name, one line): "Add a section" (opens the palette on "Blocks"), "Sketch it" (starts the Sketch tool), "From a document" (opens that feature's start). Each only when its feature is there; with one, the text is "This page is empty.".
- Drawn by the designer over the canvas, never in the page: not saved, published or in Preview. It goes when the page holds an element and returns when it is emptied. A content editor sees none.
- A drop from the palette works as today; the cards fade while something is dragged over the canvas. Tab stops in order, Enter or Space starts one.
- Under 620 px of canvas the cards stand one under the other, 250 px wide, the icon at the left.
