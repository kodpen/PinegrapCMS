Page Design Studio · the editor’s frame

Part F of plans/designer-frame-tidy.md. The frame is what stands around the four areas accepted in plan designer-panels (the Options panel, the Overview tree, the page strip, the canvas bar): the bar over the page, the heads of the left column, the bottom panel and the status bar. Those four areas are drawn here only so the frame can be read; their own drawings stay the rule for them. The editor’s chrome is always dark, so both pictures of this sheet show the same editor. Names of designs, pages and people are examples; the labels are the ones the steps write.
