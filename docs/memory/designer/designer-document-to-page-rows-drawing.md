## What does a row answer to? (F4, step FD3)
- The kind's list: on an unsure row it opens on the caption "It could be" with the reader's readings, its own ticked, then "Something else" with the other kinds the section's words can fill; a sure row's list has no captions. A kind the words cannot fill is not listed.
- Choosing a kind, the ticked one too, takes the row's mark away and puts its words back in the row.
- Opened, a row shows "Heading" and its items as fields, one line each, in the kind's shape (a card's title and text, a question and its answer, a figure and its label; a picture its alternative text, a table its cells). One row is open at a time.
- Left out: the cross strikes the heading and becomes "Put back"; the row keeps its place and the head counts "8 of 9 sections".
- Moved: the handle is dragged, a line shows where the row lands. By keyboard the handle takes the focus, the up and down arrows move the row, the new place is read out.
- None of this changes the document; "Back" and "Read" undo all of it.

## How does "Sort it for me" show its proposals? (F5, step FD4)
- While it works the button waits with "Sorting…"; the rows stay usable, and a row changed meanwhile keeps the person's choice. A line under the rows: "“Sort it for me” sends this text to Pinegrap AI. Nothing is sent before it is pressed."
- A proposal is a tinted line under its row with its own answers; the head's pill reads "2 proposals". The row stays as it is until one is pressed, then the line goes.
- A kind: "Proposed: Prices instead of Cards", "Accept" (changes the kind, takes the mark away) and "Leave". Unsure rows first; a sure row only when the words clearly fit another kind.
- A shorter text, only for a passage over 300 words: "A shorter text: 96 words in place of 420", "Show" (yours beside the proposed one, "Hide"), "Use the shorter text", "Keep mine". No button accepts all texts; a proposed text is never put in by itself.
- Ends: "Nothing to propose" ("The kinds fit the words and no passage is long."); "Pinegrap AI did not answer" ("The sections are as they were.") with "Try again". With Pinegrap AI off, or for a person who may not use it, the head has no button.

## What do the unusual states say? (F6, step FD3)
- Reading a file: a spinner, "Reading brief.docx…", the foot's button "Reading…"; a paste shows no wait. Building: "Building the page…", "Building…".
- A refusal is a red line above the form, which stays: "This file cannot be read" (only .docx, .md and .txt; an older .doc can be saved as .docx in Word), "The file is larger than 8 MB", "The file could not be opened" (damaged or protected with a password), "No text was found".
- Cut short, an amber line above the rows: "The first 2,000 paragraphs, about 64%, were read", in the reader's own figures.
- Pictures, a blue line: "6 pictures go to the file manager", and how many could not be taken (each larger than 2 MB). They are stored when the page is built, each to its place.
- No headings, a blue line: "No headings were found"; the text is one row of the kind "Text" and can be built as it is.
- At 390 px the window fills the screen and the starts are a strip; a row puts its kind and buttons on one line and its words under them; the foot's buttons stack, the filled one first.
