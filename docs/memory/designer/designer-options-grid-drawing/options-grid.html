Every label is in English as the step writes it.

This name is taken.

  • Make. A drag from one free cell to another marks the rectangle between them, with its size; a press on one cell makes an area of that cell. On release the area gets the next tint and a line in the list with its name ready to type over (“area2”).
  • Change. A press on an area or its line chooses it: the ring, a handle on its corner that is dragged to make it larger or smaller, and the bin on its line (tip “Remove area”). A press on the name edits it. A drag of the area itself moves it to free cells.
  • Refused. A rectangle that would cover another area’s cell turns red and nothing is written on release. A name is letters, digits, “-” and “_” and starts with a letter (“Use letters, digits, - and _, starting with a letter.”); a name another area has reads “This name is taken.”

These areas were drawn for 1 column at XS. LG has 3: draw them here for LG and up.
  • Small first, as everywhere. A size with no areas of its own shows the nearest smaller size’s in grey and says where they come from. The first change made there copies them to this size and changes the copy.

The keys

Tab
into the grid (one stop), then to the list, then on to Gap
← ↑ → ↓
move among the cells; an area counts as one cell
Shift + arrows
from a free cell: mark a rectangle; on a chosen area: make it larger or smaller
Enter
make an area of what is marked and type its name; on an area: edit its name
Delete
remove the chosen area
Escape
give up the mark or the name; then leave the grid
