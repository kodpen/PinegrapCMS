## Where is the drawing?
- `designer/designer-phone-preview-drawing/`: `phone-preview.html` with a `-dark.jpg` and a `-light.jpg` picture; `drawing.css` sits on top of `ui/look-sheet/sheet.css`. The designer's own parts are dark in both pictures; the phones show a site's page in its own theme.
- The sample is the design "Still Water Yoga" with the page "About us" on screen. Names, times and counts are examples; the labels are the steps'. The QR code drawn is a stand-in no camera reads.
- The window (boards H1 to H4): `designer-phone-preview-drawing-window.md`. The phone's side (boards V1 and V2): `designer-phone-preview-drawing-phone.md`.

## Which board belongs to which step?
- PP2: H1 the row in the Preview menu and the window (the link being made, ready); H2 the window's states; H3 a page for signed-in visitors, the team link, a page never saved; H4 the link in "Share prototype", the line after "End now", the narrow window.
- PP3: V1 the page with the thin line, a redraw, the page list; V2 the stop rules and what the phone says when it cannot draw.
- PP1 draws nothing: the server.

## What does the drawing add to the designer's bars and menus?
- One row, "Preview on a phone" with the phone icon, second in the Preview menu after "Preview in a new window". No button in the page bar, the canvas bar or a panel's head (`designer-light-and-simple.md`).
- The row shows where "Share the prototype…" shows; a page that cannot use it has no row.

## Which parts are drawn from what exists, and which are new?
- As today: the page toolbar, the Preview menu and its rows, a window's head and foot, the info line, the line under the canvas bar, the "Share prototype" rows.
- New: the code's box with its three stand-ins (spinner, lock, pale code), the two lines under it, the chip, and everything on the phone.
- No board: the window while "End now" is sent (the buttons go off as in "Making the link…").
