The designer's own windows and panels (the Settings window, the Options panel) are always dark, so they are dark in both pictures. Sample names, colours and counts are examples; the labels are the ones the steps write. The start questions, Export and Import are on questions-and-files.html.

  • A fold per group, one open at a time: an icon tile, the group's name, one line that says its values, a chevron. The line is written from the values, so a closed fold still tells what it holds.
  • A row per variable, 44 px high: the swatch (light half, dark half), the name, the value field for light and for dark, then “Used in n places”. A colour field is the designer's own colour field: its swatch opens the picker.
  • “Used in n places” is words, not a button: “Not used yet” in grey, “Used in 1 place”, “Used in 14 places”. The count comes with DV3; until it lands the column is left out, not drawn as zeros.
  • “Your own” lists the designer's variables under the group's fixed ones, each with a bin. A colour sits in Colour; a size sits in the fold it was added from.
  • “Add a variable” is quiet with a plus: the window's one filled button stays “Publish”. “Ask me again”, “Import” and “Export” are rows of the tab row's one quiet “More” button (A11), never buttons of their own.
  • A change redraws the canvas at once and is stored with the design: no button of its own. The foot's sentence already says it.

  • “More” is the tab row's only button: quiet, 26 px, three dots and the word. Its menu is 200 px wide, a row 30 px with an icon: “Ask me again” (C6), “Import” (D2, D3), “Export” (D1).

  • The chip stands in the field's place, 30 px high, corner 9: the accent's soft ground and line, the swatch (or the group's icon for a size), the name at 12 / 600, and “Detach” as a 22 px button at the right.
  • “Detach” turns the control back into a field holding the variable's value at that moment; nothing on the canvas moves.

  • Computed rows, not variables: a step and a multiple are worked out from “Base size”, the scale and “Base unit”. They have no “Add”, no rename and no row on the Variables tab.
  • “Type scale”: eight steps, two under the base size (“XS”, “S”) and six over it (“L” to “5XL”). The base itself is the row “Base size” above, so it is not repeated. Each row shows the size it gives now, in whole pixels.
  • “Units”: 2, 3, 4, 6 and 8 times the base unit, each with what it gives now. One unit is the row “Base unit” above.
  • The chip is the same one: “Scale 2XL”, “6 units”, with “Detach”. Changing “Base size”, the scale or “Base unit” on the Variables tab redraws every node bound to one, and the grey values of these rows.
  • The order is “Variables”, “Your own”, then the computed rows, then the way out to a typed value; a heading with no rows is left out. The search field narrows all of them.
  • The list is drawn whole. In the panel it is as high as the room under the control and scrolls inside; nothing is added to the panel's head.
