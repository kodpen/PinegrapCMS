## How is an unsure box marked while drawing?
- Nothing is asked while drawing. The box's chip carries a small amber question mark before the reader's best reading ("Blog list", "Text"); the pill "2 to check" beside the box count counts them.
- The chip's menu opens with the caption "It could be": the kinds the reader hesitates between, the best reading ticked, then "Something else" for the chip's full list.
- A row picked there settles the box early: the mark goes and the list at the build is shorter.

## What does "Build the page" open when boxes are still to check?
- One list in one step, beside the page; never one question after another. Ten unsure boxes are ten rows of one list that scrolls.
- Head: "2 boxes to check", under it "The best reading is chosen. Change what is wrong."
- A row is a box: its heading in quotes ("“Blog cards”"; "No heading" in grey for a box without one), the reason in one line, and its options as one segmented choice with the best reading already chosen and "Something else" last, which opens the chip's full list.
- The reasons: "The words name both.", "The box is empty.", "Unlike boxes side by side."
- The box is lit in amber on the canvas while its row is pointed at or focused; the row itself gets an amber edge.
- While the list is open "Build the page" in the page's head is dimmed.

## Which buttons and keys does the list have?
- "Cancel" (quiet, small) and "Build the page" (filled, small), which builds with the rows as they stand: Enter at once accepts every best reading.
- Up and Down move between the rows, Left and Right change the option.
- Escape or "Cancel" closes the list: nothing is built, and a row that was changed stays settled.

## What did the architect decide about it?
- Architect (2026-11-24): asked at "Build the page" and not while drawing; one list in one step, a row a box with its options and the best reading already chosen, the box lit on the canvas while its row is pointed at; the chip carries a quiet mark while drawing so its menu can settle it early.
