## Where is the drawing?
- `designer/designer-options-collection-drawing/`: `options-collection.html` with a `-dark.jpg` and a `-light.jpg` picture; `drawing.css` sits on top of `ui/look-sheet/sheet.css`. The designer's own parts are dark in both pictures.
- The sample is the design "Still Water Yoga": a new collection "Teachers" with five fields, and "Workshops" as a collection that is there. Names and values are examples; the labels are the step's.
- The row and the two steps: `designer-options-collection-drawing-steps.md`. What "Create" makes, the closing line, keys and measures: `designer-options-collection-drawing-made.md`.

## Which board shows what?
- N1 where it starts: the row at the foot of the Pages window's list, the same row in the command palette, a search that finds no page, a phone.
- N2 the window's second view, step 1 of 2: no name yet, a name and five fields, the kind list, the three refusals.
- N3 "One that is there", and step 2 of 2: the two pages' names and addresses, "Group", an address that is taken.
- N4 what each of the three pages holds, the lines while it is made, stopped half way.
- N5 the closing line for a new collection and for one that is there, the list with the new group, a phone.
- Z1 the keys, what is read out, the measures.

## What does the drawing add to the designer's bars?
- Nothing: no button in the Pages window's head, the page bar or the canvas bar (`designer-light-and-simple.md`). One row in the Pages window's list, one row in the command palette.

## Which parts are drawn from what exists, and which are new?
- As today: the Pages window (head with its count, "Add Page", the search, the filters, the groups, a page's row, the foot's keys, the phone's sheet: `designer-pages-window.md`), the command palette, a field, a list with its tick, the quiet and the blue small buttons, the bad line, the green "done" line.
- New: the row "New collection with pages"; the window's second view with its head (the arrow back, "1 of 2"); the two choices "A new collection" and "One that is there"; a field's line (grip, name, kind, bin) with its sub-line; a collection's line; a page's line (name and address); the lines with ticks while it is made; the closing line with links.

## What is left to the step, and asked of the architect?
- How the collection's page is closed to visitors (a folder visitors cannot open, as the templates' submission pages are, or another way) is settled with the architect when DO23 is given out. The drawing fixes only what is seen: the page is closed, staff add the items.
- The tags on the canvas ("Form list", "Form detail") are the widgets' names as today, whatever they read.
- Where "Add the first item" leads is where staff add an item to a collection today.

## Where does the drawing differ from the plan's words?
- The plan says "a row of the Pages window": the row is the list's last line, under a rule, and stays in every filter and search.
- The plan says it "asks the collection's name and fields": two steps in the window's second view, at most twelve fields there, every item's Title fixed; required, help text, rules and the slug kind are not asked.
- The plan says it "makes the collection, its list page and its detail page": the collection is a page too ("Teachers collection"), so three rows stand in the Pages window, in one group.
- The plan names no place for the pages: step 2 asks both names and addresses and the group.
- The plan says "it ends with a line naming what it made": the same line names what there is after a stop half way.
- The drawing wins where the two differ (`workflow/architect-draws-new-part.md`); what neither says the worker asks of the architect.
