## Where does it start?
- A row "New collection with pages" with the collection's icon, the last line of the Pages window's list under a rule. It stays in every filter and in every search, also when no page matches. The arrows reach it after the last page.
- The command palette has the same row; it opens the Pages window on the second view.
- Whoever sees "Add Page" sees both rows; nobody else does.
- Enter or a click turns the window to its second view: the same 440 px window, an arrow in the head that goes back to the list, the title "New collection with pages", "1 of 2".

## What does step 1 ask?
- Two choices side by side: "A new collection" (the start) and "One that is there".
- "A new collection": "Name" (the hint "Teachers, Recipes, Projects…"), then "Fields" with its count ("5 of 12").
- A field is a line: a grip, the name, the kind, the bin. Kinds: Text, Rich text, Number, Date, Image, File, Option set, Reference. "Option set" adds a sub-line "Options" (one field, a comma between them); "Reference" a sub-line "Collection" (a list of the site's collections).
- "Title", Text, is the first line from the start, locked: "Every item has a Title: it is the item's name in lists and makes its address."
- "Add field" adds a line with the pointer in its name; Enter in a name adds the next. A line is dragged by its grip; the bin removes it at once; a line without a name is dropped.
- At twelve "Add field" is off: "Twelve fields here. More are added on the collection's page afterwards."
- Required, help text, rules and the slug kind are not asked: they are set on the collection's page afterwards.
- "One that is there": every collection of the site, a line each with its fields, items and the pages that show it ("5 fields · 3 items · on no page yet"). One that has pages can be taken too. "The collection is not changed. Only its two pages are made."
- The foot reads "Nothing is made until “Create”." with "Next", off until there is a name or a chosen collection.

## What does step 2 ask?
- The collection's line on top ("Teachers", "A new collection · 5 fields") with "Change", which goes back to step 1 as "Back" and the head's arrow do.
- "Pages": "List page" and "Detail page", each a name and an address. Both names start as the collection's name; the address follows the name until one is typed by hand; the address offered is always free.
- "Group": the Pages window's group the pages go to, a new one named after the collection ("a new group") or one of the design's.
- "The list page shows the items; an item opens on the detail page. Both are pages like any other afterwards."
- The foot: "Nothing is made until “Create”.", "Back", "Create".

## What is refused, and how?
- In place, under the field, never in a window of its own.
- A collection name that is taken: "A collection named “Workshops” is there already." with "Use it", which turns to "One that is there" with that collection chosen.
- A field name used twice: "Two fields are named “Photo”."
- An address a page has: "“/classes” is the address of the page “Classes”." A page name left blank marks its field. "Create" is off until both lines are right.

## What is kept, and what is dropped?
- What was typed is kept while the Pages window is open: going back to the list, or from step 2 to step 1, loses nothing.
- Shutting the window drops it. Nothing was made, so nothing is asked.
