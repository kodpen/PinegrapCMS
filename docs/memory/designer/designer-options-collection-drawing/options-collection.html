Options: “New collection with pages”

The sheet of the plan “More options in the Options panel”, step DO23: one row of the Pages window and of the command palette that asks a collection’s name and fields, or takes a collection that is there, and makes the collection, its list page and its detail page with the widgets bound; it ends on one line naming what it made. Sample: the design “Still Water Yoga”, a collection “Teachers”. The designer’s own parts are dark in both pictures. Every label is in English as the step writes it.

  • The row sits under a line at the list’s foot, in every filter and in every search, so it is found where a page is looked for and not found. The arrows reach it after the last page; Enter or a click opens the window’s second view (board N2).
  • Who sees it: whoever sees “Add Page”. Where “Add Page” is not shown, neither the row nor the palette’s row is.
  • The palette’s row opens the Pages window on the second view. The row’s icon is the collection’s, the one its page carries in the list afterwards (board N5).

Twelve fields here. More are added on the collection’s page afterwards.

  • A field is a name and a kind: Text, Rich text, Number, Date, Image, File, Option set, Reference. “Option set” asks its options in one field, a comma between them; “Reference” asks the collection it points into. The Title is there from the start and cannot be removed or changed.
  • “Add field” adds a line under the last with the pointer in its name; Enter in a name adds the next. A line is dragged by its grip to its place; the bin removes it at once. A line left without a name is dropped.
  • “Next” is off until the collection has a name. Whether a field is required, its help text, its rules and the slug kind are not asked here: they are set on the collection’s page afterwards, as today.
  • Refused in place: a collection name that is taken, with “Use it”, which turns to “One that is there” with that collection chosen (board N3); a field name used twice. Nothing is said in a window of its own.
  • What was typed is kept while the Pages window is open: going back to the list, or to this step from the next, loses nothing. Shutting the window drops it; nothing was made.

  • “One that is there” lists every collection of the site with its count of fields and items and the pages that show it. One that has pages can be taken too: two more pages are made.
  • The names start as the collection’s name for both pages; the operator shortens the detail page’s (“Teacher”). The addresses follow the names as they are typed until an address is typed by hand; the one offered is always free.
  • “Group” is the Pages window’s group the pages go to: a new one named after the collection, or one of the design’s.
  • Refused in place: an address a page has, a page name left blank. “Create” is off until both lines are right.
  • “Change” on the collection’s line goes back to step 1, as “Back” and the head’s arrow do.

  • The pages take the design’s look: its header and footer, its styles, the widgets’ own start. Nothing is styled by this window; everything is changed on the canvas afterwards, as on a page made with “Add Page”.

  • The links: each page’s name opens that page in the designer; the collection’s name opens the collection’s page. The line stays until “Done” or until the window is shut, and is read out once.

Keys, what is read out, measures Step DO23

Z1 · Keys, what is read out, measuresStep DO23

Keys

Ctrl P
The Pages window. ↓ past the last page reaches the row; Enter opens the second view.
Ctrl Shift P
The command palette: “New collection with pages”.
Tab
The two choices, “Name”, then each field’s name, kind and bin, “Add field”, “Next”. In step 2: the collection’s “Change”, the two names and addresses, “Group”, “Back”, “Create”.
← →
On the two choices: the other one.
Enter
In a field’s name: a new field under it. On a collection’s line: chooses it. On “Next”, “Create”, “Done”: the button.
Alt ↑ ↓
Moves the field one place up or down.
Esc
Shuts an open list; else one step back: from step 2 to step 1, from step 1 to the list of pages. On the closing line it does what “Done” does.

  • The window is the Pages window: 440 px, its head, its fields and its rows of 32 px as today; the phone’s sheet as today. The second view keeps the window’s place and grows down with the fields, then scrolls inside.
