Show everything
  • Person: “Everyone”, “Me”, then the people named in the rows read so far, then “Customers” (the answers and comments on share links) and “The schedule”. A content editor has no “Customers”.
  • Page: “Every page”, the page that is open with “open now”, then the design’s pages by name, with a search field above them. A page deleted for good is in no menu; its rows show under “Every page”.
  • Kind: “Everything”, then the reader’s six kinds in these words: Page versions, Pages on and off the site (the bin, the schedule), Sharing, The design, Shared components, Design files. A content editor has the first two.
  • Narrowing is the reader’s: the three go to the reader with the request, so a narrowed list is still fifty at a time from the whole design, never a filter over the rows already on the screen.
  • A chooser that narrows is blue and shows its choice in place of its name; choosing the first row of its menu clears it. Nothing is remembered: History opens with all three open wide.
  • Narrowed to nothing: the list’s place says so, with “Show everything”, which clears all three.

This is where the design’s activity begins.
  • The window is Compare’s as History opens it for a revision (designer/designer-compare-live-drawing.md, board L6), drawn here only for what the row hands it: left the revision before, right the row’s revision. Nothing in the window is new.
  • Any page of the design: the row’s page need not be the one that is open; both sides are read as revisions. “Load this revision” is offered only when the page is the open one, as History’s rule is today.
  • Closing gives History back on “Activity”, with its choosers and its place in the list kept.
  • More rows: near the end of what is read, the next fifty are asked for with the reader’s cursor; a quiet line with a spinner stands at the end meanwhile. A failed read puts “Try again” in that line and keeps the rows.
  • The last row: one grey line, “This is where the design’s activity begins.” What the site no longer keeps (the audit trail’s years, designer/designer-activity-audit-trail.md) is simply not there; the line is the same.
  • A row whose version is gone (swept by the revisions’ retention) is in no list: the reader gives only what the stores still hold.
