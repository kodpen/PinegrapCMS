## What is the window?
- 360 px wide, head "Preview on a phone" with the cross. Centred: the code (196 px on white), "Point your phone's camera here", then "Starts on About us · works for 7 h 58 min".
- Under it one blue info line: "The phone shows the pages as last saved and redraws after each save."
- Foot: "End now" (ghost) on the left, "Copy link" (quiet, with the copy icon) on the right. No filled button: the window confirms nothing.
- Focus starts on "Copy link". Escape or the cross closes the window and the link lives on.
- The first press makes the link: a spinner in the code's place, "Making the link…", both buttons off. Later presses show the same code while the link lives.

## Which states does the sheet settle?
- Changes not saved: an amber line in the info line's place, "You have changes that are not saved. The phone shows them after a save.", with "Save" (quiet, small), the designer's own Save.
- "Copy link" reads "Copied" with a tick for two seconds.
- The time left is words ("7 h 58 min", "42 min", "less than a minute"), counted down once a minute in the open window.
- Run out while open: the code pale, "This link has ended", "The phone now shows “This link no longer works.”", and "Make a new link" (filled, small).
- Not made: "The link could not be made", "Nothing was changed.", "Try again" (quiet, small).
- "End now" asks nothing: the window closes and one line under the canvas bar says "The phone preview has ended."

## What about a page the link cannot show?
- The page on screen is for signed-in visitors: no code yet, a lock, "“My courses” is for signed-in visitors", "A link for anyone cannot show it. A team link opens after a sign-in on the phone.", and "Make a team link" (filled, small). Foot: "Team: administrators, designers and managers."
- The team link's window carries the chip "Team link" and "Sign in on the phone to open it." The two links can live side by side.
- The page on screen was never saved: the link starts on the first page, and a blue line says "“New page” has not been saved yet. Save it and it joins the phone's page list."

## Where else does the link show?
- In today's "Share prototype" list, unchanged but for the chip "Phone preview" on its row; "Revoke" there is "End now". The phone's link takes no password, comments or approval.
- Below 768 px the window fills the screen: "Point another phone's camera here", "Open here" (filled, opens the link in a new tab), "Copy link", "End now".
