## What do the Text "More" rows hold?
- At the end of the Options panel's "Text" section (`propsTextOptions()`, `_sdTxRowsHtml()`), a quiet "More" row as in "Shadow and effect"; open, sub-head "Text effect": "Text shadow" (None, Soft, Medium, Strong, Glow), "Letter spacing" (slider −0.1 to 0.5 em with its number), "Font family" (Default, Body font, Heading font, each with the design's family name), "Limit to lines" (1 to 10), "Gradient fill" (None and four pairs of theme colours), "Outline" (width 0 to 4 px and a theme colour).
- "More" opens by itself while one of them holds a value. Shown for one element whose custom style is on (`_sdStateNode()`).
