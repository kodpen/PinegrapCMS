## Where is the drawing?
- `designer/designer-sketch-drawing/`: `build.html` (part A), `details.html` (part B) and `ai-and-picture.html` (part C), each with a `-dark.jpg` and a `-light.jpg` picture; the sheets share `drawing.css` on top of `ui/look-sheet/sheet.css`.
- The designer's frame is drawn as it is today, dark in both pictures; only the sheet around it follows the theme.
- Sample pages, names and counts are examples; the labels are the steps'. Where the plan and a sheet differ the sheet wins (`workflow/architect-draws-new-part.md`).
- Part A: `designer-sketch-drawing-build.md` (the two actions, the "More" menu, the palette, the built page), `designer-sketch-drawing-check.md` (the unsure boxes: the chip's mark and the one list at the build) and `designer-sketch-drawing-build-again.md` (the "Build again" question, what goes wrong, narrow).
- Part B: `designer-sketch-drawing-details.md`. Part C: `designer-sketch-drawing-ai.md` (the two AI actions) and `designer-sketch-drawing-picture.md` (the reference picture and "Read the picture").

## Which board belongs to which step?
- SK4, part A: B1 the Prototype view with a sketch page selected, B2 the "More" menu, B3 the command palette rows and the first-use line, B4 the unsure boxes marked and asked in one list, B5 busy and the built page, B6 the "Build again" question, B7 nothing to build, boxes left out, a failed and a refused build, B8 zoomed out and the question on a phone.
- SK5, part B: D1 "Page details" as it first opens, D2 filled, D3 a value that cannot be saved, the save in progress and the read-only window, D4 on a phone. SK5 adds its row to the menu of B2.
- SK6, part C: E1 the "Describe the page" window, E2 the sketch being drawn and drawn, E3 "Write the texts", E6 the window on a phone and Pinegrap AI turned off. SK6 adds the two rows under "Pinegrap AI" to the menu of B2.
- SK7, part C: E4 the reference picture under a sketch page and its rows in "More", E5 "Read the picture".
- SK8, the feel round, draws nothing: it measures what the other steps built.

## What does the drawing add to the designer's bars?
- Nothing. No button enters the page bar, the canvas bar or a panel's head (`designer-light-and-simple.md`).
- Every action of SK4 to SK7 is in the head of the selected sketch page: "Build the page" and the "More" menu. The same rows are in the command palette.

## Which parts are drawn from a Design example?
- The "Build again" question is "Confirm with its effects" with its three kinds of row (keep, info, go); its tile is blue and its button filled, not the danger button: the build removes a section but History keeps the page.
- A message under a frame is "Result line", its tile's tone changing (teal done, blue nothing to do, amber left out, rose failed or refused).
- The rows of "Page details" are made like the Settings window's rows.
- The "More" menu and the chip's question are the designer's own menus of today; "n to check" is the amber mark the map's count uses; the first-use line is the one `designer-light-and-simple.md` asks for.
