  • The window is at most 1120 × 720 px and stands in the middle of the screen; below that it keeps 24 px from each edge. One height whatever the start, as today.
  • The rail is 256 px. A row is one icon tile (32 px), the start’s name and one short line that never wraps. The small drawings leave: nobody could read them at that size.
  • Three groups with a grey label each: “Start new”, “From a design tool”, “From what you have”. The order of the starts inside a group is today’s.
  • The pane’s head is the chosen start’s icon as a 44 px accent tile, a title that says the act and one sentence of what happens next. It never repeats the row’s line.
  • “Built on” is a label over two choice cards named as things: “Bootstrap 5” and “Custom”. Each has one line and three ticks of what it brings; the long paragraphs leave. The pane’s content is at most 720 px wide.
  • The foot has “Cancel” (quiet) and one filled button. It reads “Create design” on Blank, ZIP file and Pasted markup, “Use this template” on Template; the import starts keep their own words.
