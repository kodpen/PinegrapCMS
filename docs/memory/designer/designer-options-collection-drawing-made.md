## What does "Create" make, and in which order?
- The collection, its list page, its detail page. With a collection that is there, the two pages only.
- The collection is a page whose form is the collection, as every collection is (`collections-decisions.md`): one control a field, by its kind. It is named after the collection with "collection" after it ("Teachers collection", "/teachers-collection") and is made closed to visitors, so staff add the items. No table or column is added.
- The list page: a heading with the collection's name and the form list widget bound to the collection, showing the Title, the first image and the first text or option field of each item, each linked to the detail page.
- The detail page: the form detail widget bound to the collection, every field in the order of step 1 (the Title as the heading, an image as a picture, a reference as the name of what it points at).
- The pages take the design's look; the window styles nothing, and everything is changed on the canvas afterwards.
- All three go to the group chosen in step 2; the collection's page carries the collection's icon and a lock in its row.

## What is shown while it is made?
- The head loses its arrow; three lines, one for each thing, with a tick when made and a turning mark on the one in hand; the foot reads "Making…" and "Create" is off.
- Stopped half way: the line that failed gets a cross and "not made"; a bad line says why ("The detail page was not made: the connection was lost. What was made stays."). "Try again" makes only what is missing; "Close" leaves it so.

## What is the closing line?
- One green line, each name a link: "Made the collection “Teachers” with 5 fields, the list page “Teachers” and the detail page “Teacher”."
- For a collection that is there: "Made the list page “Workshops” and the detail page “Workshop” for the collection “Workshops”."
- After a stop half way and "Close" the same line names what there is.
- A page's name opens that page in the designer; the collection's name opens the collection's page.
- Under it, for a collection without items: "The list is empty until the collection has an item." and "Add the first item" (a new tab, where staff add an item today). "Done" turns the window back to the list, where the pages stand in their group.
- No undo step is written, as "Add Page" writes none. The three are pages like any other: renamed, moved or deleted from the row's menu.

## Which keys?
- Ctrl+P, then Down past the last page and Enter; or Ctrl+Shift+P and the row.
- Tab: the two choices, "Name", each field's name, kind and bin, "Add field", "Next"; in step 2 "Change", the names and addresses, "Group", "Back", "Create".
- Left and Right change the choice. Enter in a field's name adds a field; on a collection's line it chooses it. Alt+Up and Alt+Down move a field.
- Esc shuts an open list, else goes one step back (step 2, step 1, the list); on the closing line it does what "Done" does.

## What is read out?
- "New collection with pages, button"; "New collection with pages, step 1 of 2"; "Field 2 of 5, Photo, Image"; a moved field "Photo, place 3 of 5".
- A refusal when it appears; "Making the collection, the list page and the detail page"; the closing line as written, once.

## Which measures?
- The window is the Pages window (440 px, 32 px rows); the second view grows down with the fields, then scrolls inside. The phone uses the sheet.
- The row is 30 px high, as "N more pages", under a 1 px rule.
- A field's line: grip 14 px, name and kind 5 to 4, bin 26 px; a sub-line is set in 20 px. The kind list is 190 px wide, the kind's icon before each name.
