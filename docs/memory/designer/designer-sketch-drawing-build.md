## Where do "Build the page" and "More" stand?
- In the head of the selected sketch page only: "Build the page" (filled, small) and "More" (the three dots, title "More"). Another page selected, or Sketch off, and they are gone.
- Beside the box count an amber pill "n to check" counts the boxes the reader is not sure of; the chip of each carries a small amber question mark before its kind.
- The two actions keep their size on the screen at every zoom.

## What does "More" hold?
- "Page details"; "Reference picture"; under the caption "Pinegrap AI": "Describe the page" and "Write the texts"; then "Delete the page" with its key "Del".
- SK4 opens the menu with its first rows; SK5, SK6 and SK7 each add their own.
- With Pinegrap AI off the caption and its two rows are not in the menu at all.
- "Write the texts" is dimmed until the page is built; its title says "Build the page first".

## What does the command palette list?
- The same rows for the selected sketch page, each naming the page at its right ("Pinegrap AI" for the two AI rows); with no sketch page selected they are not listed.

## What is the first-use line?
- Once, on the first sketch page that has a box: "“Build the page” turns these boxes into real sections of a draft page, with sample content you then edit." with "Got it" (quiet, small).
- "Got it" closes it for good; so does the first build.

## How is a box the reader is not sure of asked?
- While drawing nothing is asked: the chip's mark and the pill only say so. At "Build the page" all of them are asked in one list in one step (`designer-sketch-drawing-check.md`).

## What does the build look like while it runs?
- The head shows a spinner and "Building the page…" in place of the two actions; the boxes do not take the pointer. Nothing to stop: the build is one save.

## What is the built page?
- A page like the others (blue when selected, arrows to and from it) with "Draft" in its head. Sketch on shows its boxes, Sketch off its picture.
- Header and Footer chips carry the link mark: they are the design's shared components.
- "Build again" (quiet, small) takes the place of "Build the page", dimmed until the sketch differs from what was built.
- Message under the frame: "**Page 7** is built: 7 sections, as a draft." with "Open the page" (quiet), which goes to Design on that page.
