## What is measured?
- A drag (draw, move, resize) on a sketch page of a hundred boxes in the Prototype view, in Chromium on the sandbox: frames a second and the longest frame, before and after.
- Touch and pen drags, every key of `prototype-sketch-tool.md`, and the look in the light and dark themes.
