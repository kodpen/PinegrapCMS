## How does the Canva start open?
- No connection, no account, no link field: the pane is an upload. Canva has no page and no row on Connections.
- Pane title "Start from Canva pages" and one sentence: download the pages from Canva as PNG or PDF and add them; each comes in as a picture to build on, a model for the page, not layers that can be edited. It is not a warning box and has no "Got it".
- A drop place: "Drop PNG or PDF files here", the line "In Canva: Share, Download, then “PNG” or “PDF Standard”.", and the quiet button "Choose files" (several files at once).
- Foot: "Cancel" quiet, "Next" filled; grey until one page is there.
- On a touch screen the place reads "Add PNG or PDF files" and the whole place is the button.

## How are the added pages shown?
- Head "n pages" with "from n files". A tile per page, four a row: the picture, its place as a number at the top left, a bin at the top right, the page's name and the file's name.
- A PDF is unfolded into its pages, named "<file>, page n"; a PNG takes its file's name. The name becomes the page's name in the design.
- Dragging a tile changes the order. The bin takes a page out without a question.
- The drop place shrinks to one row under the tiles, "Drop more files here", with "Choose files".
- Foot: the hint "Each picture becomes the model of one page.", "Back" ghost, "Next" filled.

## What do busy and refused files look like?
- A file is a row while it is read: its icon, name, "Reading the pages, n of n" and a bar, with a cross that stops it. "Next" waits while a file is read.
- A refused file is a red row with its reason and a cross that clears it; the other files are left alone.
- The reasons: not a PNG or a PDF ("Download the page from Canva as one of the two."); larger than the site's upload limit; a PDF with more pages than one design takes, where the first ones were added.
- The size is the site's upload limit and the most pictures of one design is the number ST6 sets; the sheet's 32 MB and 20 are examples.

## What comes in place of the start questions?
- The same card as the Figma start's, "Your design's variables": "Brand colour" and "Second colour" as rows of the colours measured from the pictures, the answer ringed; a press on another swatch changes it in place, with no picker.
- The third row, "Type, feel, room", reads "The site's default": a picture carries no fonts.
- Foot: "Back" ghost, "Ask me the questions" quiet (DV4's five questions with the two colours chosen), "Create design" filled.
- "Create design" opens the design in the Sketch tool, each page with its picture as the reference under the frames (the sketch sheet, step SK7). There is no report: a picture is either added or refused.

## What changes at 390 px?
- The window is the full screen with the starts as a strip; the tiles go two a row; the drop row keeps a short "Choose" button; the foot's buttons stack full width.
