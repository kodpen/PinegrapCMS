## Where is the drawing?
- `designer/designer-variables-drawing/questions-and-files.html` with its `-dark.jpg` and `-light.jpg` picture: parts C and D. The folder, the other sheet and the Variables tab itself: `designer-variables-drawing.md`.

## Which board belongs to which step?
- DV4, part C: C1 the first question in the New design window, C2 to C5 the other four, C6 "Ask me again" inside the Theme pane.
- DV5, part D: D1 the result line of "Export", D2 "Import" refused with its reason, D3 the question that lists what a file would change.

## How are the start questions drawn?
- In the New design window after the start's own form: a step line of five ("Brand colour", "Second colour", "Feel", "Type", "Room"), one question at a time; foot "Back" (ghost), "Skip" (quiet), "Next" (filled; "Create design" on the fifth).
- Answers are choice cards, the chosen one with an accent ring and a tick. Feel offers six looks and "Show all 14 looks"; the four type feels on the sheet are examples, DV4 lists the pairs the site's fonts allow.
- Every question can be skipped: a skipped step stays a grey number in the step line, and skipping all five gives the design as it would open today.

## How is "Ask me again" drawn?
- A row of the Variables tab's "More" menu. It shows the same questions inside the Theme pane in place of the folds: "Cancel", "Skip", "Next" (quiet with the accent's line; "Use these answers" on the fifth). No button there is filled: the window's "Publish" is.
- The answers replace the four groups' values; your own variables and bound controls stay. "Cancel" goes back to the folds with nothing changed; the canvas redraws once, when the answers are used.

## How do Export and Import answer?
- Both are rows of the "More" menu. "Export" saves the file at once and shows a teal line under the tab row that says how many variables went out and to which file.
- "Import" opens the browser's file box, then a question listing how many values change, how many come as your own and how many are left out, with "Import" filled and "Cancel" quiet.
- A refused file changes nothing: a red line under the tab row with the file's name, the reason and "Choose another file".
