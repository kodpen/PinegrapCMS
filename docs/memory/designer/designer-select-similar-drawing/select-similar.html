  • Where the row stands. On the canvas under “Select Parent”, with the rows that change what is chosen. In Overview first in the group of “Move…”, “Duplicate” and “Delete”. Icon bi-ui-checks-grid, the key S at the row’s end.
  • When it is there. For one chosen element that is not the page itself. With several chosen the menus have no such row and the key does nothing; the palette’s row is offered only while one element is chosen and names its kind at the right.
  • The key. S with one element chosen and no field in use, as N, M and P work today. Pressing the row or the key chooses at once, with no window and no question (board E2).
  • Nothing runs before the press. No count beside the row, no frame while the pointer is on it: the page is walked when the row is pressed.
  • Not in the bars. No button in the page bar, the canvas bar or the panel’s head, and no row in the Options panel’s three-dot menu.
  • Read out. The row is a menu item named “Select similar”; its key is read with it.

  • What is chosen. The elements of the same kind with the same classes inside the chosen one’s section: the twelve cards div.card.h-100.shadow-sm. The dark card under them has other classes; the three cards of “Teachers” stand in another section.
  • On the canvas. Each is framed as several chosen elements are framed today; the one the press started from keeps its name tag. The canvas does not scroll.
  • The line. First in the panel, above the identity row, 36 px high: the count in bold, then the two lists as words with a small arrow. It stays while what is chosen is what the line found, and goes when the selection is cleared or another element is clicked.
  • Under it the panel of several elements as it is: the icon with the count, “12 elements selected”, the kind, the tabs, “A change is written to all 12 elements at once”, “— Multiple —” where they differ. One change is one undo step.
  • First use. One line under the line, once: “Every element like the one you chose is selected. Change something once and all of them change.” with “Got it”. It is never shown again after “Got it” or after the first change.
  • Undo. Choosing is not an undo step. Escape clears the selection as today, and the line with it.

  • Alike by. “Same kind and classes” (the start), “Same kind”, “One of its classes” and, only when the chosen one is a placed shared component, “The same shared component”: the ways the matcher’s ways() gives, in its order.
  • Where. “Beside it” (the same parent), “In this section” (the start), “On this page”. A page with no section reads “In this section” as the whole page.
  • The number at a row’s right is how many that row would choose, the chosen one counted, with the other list left as it is. A row that finds no other element reads “none” and is dimmed; it cannot be chosen.
  • The line’s words. “same kind and classes”, “same kind”, “class card” (the class’s name as written), “same shared component”; then “beside it”, “in this section” or “on this page”. So: “13 similar · class card · in this section”.
  • One of its classes opens a second list beside the row with each class of the chosen element and its count. An element with no class has no such row; with one class the row chooses it without a second list.
  • The lists are counted when one is opened, not before: the page is walked once for each row, a few milliseconds each on the fixture page. Nothing is remembered: the next press starts again from “same kind and classes · in this section”.
  • Keys. The two words are the panel’s first two Tab stops; Enter or Space opens a list, the arrows move, Enter chooses, Escape closes the list and keeps the selection.
  • Read out. After the press and after each choice: “12 similar elements selected: same kind and classes, in this section.” A list’s row reads its count: “Same kind, 13”.
  • Width. A list is as wide as its longest row, at most 252 px, and opens under its word; near the panel’s right edge it is moved left to stay inside the window.

  • Taking one out. Ctrl and a click on a chosen element takes it out as it does today; the line stays and counts “11 of 12 similar”. Ctrl and a click on an element the line did not find makes it an ordinary selection of several: the line goes.
  • Left out, never chosen: an element the person may not edit (“you may not edit”), the inside of a placed shared component (“inside a shared component”), a system widget’s own parts (“a system widget’s own parts”). A second row of the line says how many of each, in that order, only the ones that are not zero.
  • The count never includes what is left out, in the line and in a list’s row alike. With nothing left out the second row is not drawn.
  • Nothing found. The chosen element stays chosen with its own panel. The line says “No other element like this one here”, keeps the two lists and offers the next wider place as a button: “Look in this section” from “beside it”, “Look on this page” from “in this section”.
  • Nothing on the page either: “No other element like this one on this page”, no button; “Alike by” can still be widened. This line goes with the next click, as the other does.
  • Narrow. When the line does not fit one row the count keeps the first row and the two lists go under it, side by side when they fit and one under the other when they do not; a dot is drawn only between parts that share a row. The left-out row wraps under them. Nothing is cut short.
