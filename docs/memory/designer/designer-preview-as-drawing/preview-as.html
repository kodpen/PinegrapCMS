Hotspots
  • The bar is 40px high and pushes the page down; it never lies over the page. From the left: the eye mark with Preview as, then Record, Visitor, Case. Nothing stands at the right until a choice is made.
  • A chooser is a quiet menu button: its name in grey, the value drawn now in white, a caret. Record shows only on a page with a single-record widget, Case only on a page with more than “Typical”; Visitor always shows. A chooser the page cannot use is left out, never shown dead.
  • As it opens nothing is chosen: the record is the one today's Preview draws (the newest submission, the first product), the visitor is “Signed out”, the case “Typical”.
  • The first-use line shows once for each person and goes with Got it (a quiet small button). The same place under the bar carries every later line (P5, P6): one line at a time.
  • “Hotspots” stays where it is today, at the page's lower left.
  • Keyboard: Tab goes through the choosers from the left; Enter, Space or Down opens one; Escape closes it and the focus returns to the chooser.

“Offer form” has no submission yet.

  • The menu is 320px wide: a search field with the focus in it, a caption naming whose records these are, then the rows. A row is the record's name with one grey detail at its right (a product's price, a submission's date, an event's day).
  • The ticked row is the one drawn now. The record today's Preview draws carries the grey note shown by default; choosing it is the same as “Reset” for this chooser.
  • The first rows come without typing; the grey foot says how many there are. Typing searches by name after a short pause, the list is replaced by Searching… and then by what was found. A widget with no record yet says so in one line, and the page is drawn as today.

Hotspots
  • While the page is drawn the old one stays, dimmed, with a small spinner and Drawing… at the bar's right; the bar itself is never locked and a new choice replaces the one on its way.
  • Reset puts the three choosers back to what the Preview draws by default and leaves; it shows only while one of them differs.
  • The choice is kept for this page in this browser: closing the window and opening the Preview again shows the same record, visitor and case. Nothing of it is saved with the design, and another page starts at its own default.
  • A draw that fails leaves the last good page and one rose line with Try again (quiet small). A kept record that is gone falls back to the default with one amber line that the X closes.
  • The live refresh after an edit keeps the bar as it is: the page under it is drawn again with the same choices, and an open menu is not closed by it.
  • No line here ever names a record the person may not open: such a kept record reads as no longer there.

  • While it runs the three choosers show the case on its way and are dimmed; the page under them is each case as it is drawn. At the right a small spinner with Checking 3 of 6… and Stop (quiet small).
  • The cases are the page's own: each case of “Case” for each visitor the page has a rule for. A page with one case has no such row.
  • The list is 300px wide and lies over the page's right edge with a shadow; it never narrows the page, so what was measured is what a visitor sees at this width. Its foot says the width that was checked.
  • A row is the case and the visitor, then the finding with its amber mark and the name of the place: A text leaves its box, A picture is missing, A widget draws nothing. Only cases with a finding are listed.
  • A row opens its case: the choosers change to it, the page is drawn, scrolled to the place, and the place is outlined in amber until the next click. When the place lies under the list, the list moves to the left edge. The list stays until its X.
  • With nothing found there is no list: one teal line under the bar, Every case looks right. with the number drawn. “Stop” keeps what was found so far and says Stopped after 3 of 6.
  • A case that fails is a row of its own in rose with Try again; the others are still checked.
  • Check again (quiet small) runs it once more, for example after an edit; the list is not kept when the window closes.
  • Keyboard: Down and Up move through the rows, Enter opens the case, Escape closes the list. From the palette the Preview window opens first when it is closed.
