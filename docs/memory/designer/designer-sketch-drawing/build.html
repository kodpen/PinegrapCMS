  • While drawing nothing is asked. An unsure box's chip carries the small amber question mark and 2 to check counts them. Its menu opens with It could be: the kinds the reader hesitates between, the best reading ticked, then Something else for the full list. A row picked there settles the box early: the mark goes and the list at the build is shorter.
  • Build the page with boxes to check opens one list in one step, beside the page: a row a box, with its heading, the reason in one line (“The words name both.”, “The box is empty.”, “Unlike boxes side by side.”) and its options, the best reading already chosen. Something else opens the chip's full list. The box is lit in amber on the canvas while its row is pointed at or focused. Ten unsure boxes are ten rows of one list that scrolls, never ten steps.
  • Cancel (quiet, small), Build the page (filled, small) builds with the rows as they stand, so Enter at once accepts every best reading. Up and Down move between the rows, Left and Right change the option. Escape or Cancel closes the list: nothing is built, a row that was changed stays settled.

  • A section is matched to its box. One that was edited in Design is never rebuilt on its own: it stays, also when its box moved or changed size. Teal rows stay, blue rows are built, the rose row goes.
  • More lists each edited section whose box changed, with a tick box, off, to build that one again from the sketch. Without such a section “More” is not shown.
  • Cancel (quiet), Build again (filled). Enter builds, Escape cancels. A History entry of the page is written before the build runs, with or without the question; the foot's note leans on it, and a build whose entry could not be written does not start. With nothing removed and no edited section changed there is no question: the page is built again at once and the message says what changed.
