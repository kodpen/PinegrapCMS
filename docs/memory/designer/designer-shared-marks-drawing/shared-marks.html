  • Today. A shared component wears a green ring of 3 px outside and 3 px inside, on its wrapper and again on each direct child; a system widget a 2 px indigo border. Always, pointer or not.
  • From now on nothing is drawn at rest. No ring, no border, no tint, no tag: a person looking over the designer’s shoulder sees the page, not the tool’s marks.
  • Sizes are the site’s. The 2 px border made a system widget 4 px wider and taller on the canvas than on the site; with it gone a cart or a login button sits where the site puts it.
  • What still tells them apart at a glance: the Overview’s rows keep their colours (board Q4), the pointer shows a part’s frame the moment it is over it (Q2), and one switch lights them all (Q3).
  • Kept as they are: the dashed stand-in of a component that is empty, still loading or a loop’s template, and the frame Outline draws round every element.
  • The colours stay: green #10b981 for a shared component, indigo #6366f1 for a system widget, blue for an element of the page.

  • The pointer on a shared component: a frame of 1.5 px in green on its box and a green name tag: the puzzle icon, its name, “Shared”. On a system widget the same in indigo with the chip icon and “System”.
  • Only the innermost part under the pointer gets a name tag, as with every element today. A shared component or widget the pointer is inside of, but not on, shows a thin 1 px line of its colour, so it is plain that the part belongs to it.
  • Chosen: the frame is 2 px in the part’s colour and the tag gains the grip, as the blue tag of an element does. The line of the component around a chosen element stays while that element is chosen.
  • The tag’s place follows today’s rule for an element’s tag: above the frame, inside its top edge when the part touches the canvas’s top, under it for a small part in a bar. It never pushes anything.
  • The words in the tag replace “[Shared] Header” and “[System] Cart”: “Header · Shared”, “Cart · System”. The second word is lighter. Both are translated texts.
  • No room taken. A frame is drawn over the part and never changes its size or the place of what is beside it, also on a part whose wrapper has no box of its own (a button group, a menu, an input group): there the frame goes on the child, as the ring does today.
  • The warning stays where it is: the first change inside a shared component shows today’s line once per component and visit. It is the moment that matters, and it does not depend on a frame having been noticed.
  • A person who may change content only: the same frame and tag under the pointer, no grip; what they may not change keeps today’s “not allowed” pointer and its reason.
  • Keys. Nothing new: a part reached with the keyboard is chosen, so it wears the chosen frame and its tag; the tag’s words are what a screen reader hears for it.

  • The row. “Shared parts”, the puzzle icon, second in the View menu under “Outline”; a switch like the others, counted in the menu button’s badge. In the command palette: “Show shared parts”, and “Hide shared parts” while it is on.
  • On: every shared component wears a soft green wash, a 1 px line and its name tag inside its top left corner; every system widget a soft indigo wash and a 1 px line, its tag only under the pointer (a widget is often too small to carry one).
  • Still lighter than today: a wash and a hairline, no 3 px ring, nothing doubled on the children, no size changed. The pointer and the chosen frames of board Q2 work the same over it.
  • Off at first, for everyone. It is remembered per person and browser where the other View switches are kept, so a designer who wants the parts lit keeps them lit.
  • Not in Preview, not in a picture of the page, not in Prototype mode: it is a help for building and belongs to the canvas of Design mode only.
  • No button in the page bar, the canvas bar or a panel’s head, and no key of its own (designer/designer-light-and-simple.md).
