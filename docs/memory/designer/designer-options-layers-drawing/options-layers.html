Options: background layers, Clip shape and Mask

The sheet of the plan “More options in the Options panel”, steps DO13 and DO14: a background as a list of layers (colour, gradient, picture), a gradient with three or more stops taken from the palette, and an element cut by a Clip shape or a Mask. Sample: the hero section of the page “Home”, design “Still Water Yoga”. The designer’s own parts are dark in both pictures. Every label is in English as the steps write it.

8 layers, the most an element takes Add layer
  • A list in the section, nothing else. A sub-head, one line a layer (grip, a small picture of the layer, its kind, a summary) and “Add layer” under it. No window, no button in the panel’s head or in a bar.
  • Front first. The first line is drawn over the others, as the Layers tab orders elements. “Background Color” above the list is the theme colour under every layer; a “Colour” layer is a colour of its own with its own opacity.
  • Read before written. An element with one background (a custom colour, a two-colour gradient, a picture) shows it as one layer and is written back as it was until a layer is changed, added, removed or moved.

  • The strip is the gradient. It shows the stops in a row whatever the type; a handle under it for each stop, dragged to move it, the chosen one ringed. A press on a free place adds a stop of the colour found there; the bin beside “Stop 2 of 3” removes the chosen one. Two stops is the least, sixteen the most.
  • A palette colour follows the palette. A stop’s “Color” lists the design’s colours and then “Custom…”. A palette colour is written as the theme’s variable and carries the link mark: when the palette changes, the gradient changes. Its “Opacity” is the stop’s own.
  • Type keeps the stops. Linear has “Angle”; Radial “Shape” (Circle, Ellipse) and “Center”; Conic “Start” and “Center”. “Center” is the nine places Transform’s Origin uses.

Enlarged, and the keys Step DO13

Z1 · The strip and its stops; the keysStep DO13 · The strip three times enlarged.

Three stops, the second chosen

Strip 18 px high, corner 6 px, on the checked ground that shows see-through. Handle 12 px with its point, in the stop’s colour; the chosen one ringed. Line 34 px, the small picture 22 px.

The keys

Tab
to the list (one stop), then into the open layer’s rows; the strip is one stop
↑ ↓
in the list: from layer to layer
Alt + ↑ ↓
move the layer forward or back
Enter
open or close the layer; on the strip: add a stop in the widest gap
← →
on the strip: from stop to stop; with Shift, move the stop by 1 %
Delete
remove the layer, or the chosen stop
  • Read out. The list: “Background layers, 3”. A line: “Gradient, linear 180 degrees, 2 stops, layer 1 of 3”. A stop: “Stop 2 of 3, Primary, 45 per cent opacity, at 55 per cent”. A move, an add and a removal are announced.
  • Undo. Each layer added, removed or moved and each stop added or removed is one undo step; a drag is one step from press to release.
  • No new button in the panel’s head, the page bar or the canvas bar: a list in Color and background, two rows under Shadow and effect’s “More”.
