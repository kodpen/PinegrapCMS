## How is a .docx or .xlsx shown?
- Read on the server by `includes/fn/office.php` (PR #530) and drawn by `assets/js/pg_office_view.js`, fetched on first use. No library: a worksheet is walked with XMLReader a row at a time (the way `import_products_f.php` reads one), a document with DOMDocument. A workbook comes back as sheets of rows of strings (`pg_office_xlsx_read()`: every sheet in the workbook's order, hidden ones flagged, shared and inline strings, booleans, dates and times by the cell's style (`office-xlsx-cell-values.md`), numbers without exponent, days counted from 1904 where the workbook says so, the sheet's own row numbers so gaps stay, and per row `calc`: the columns holding a formula with no stored result, which the viewer marks ƒx); a document as a small safe subset of HTML (`pg_office_docx_read()`: headings by style id, paragraphs with bold/italic/underline/strike/super/sub, alignment, bullet and numbered lists from the paragraph or its style, tables with column spans, http(s)/mailto links, pictures inlined as data: URLs, tabs, non-breaking and optional hyphens, page breaks; deleted tracked text left out). Everything the file wrote is escaped; nothing passes through as markup.
- Doors: the file manager's `explorer_office_preview` (file window and Quick Look, `check_view_access()` on the folder; an ERP document to whoever may read the ERP) and the Workspace's `ws_file_office` (the preview window, a Preview button on the file card and in the Files tab; channel readers). The file payload carries `office: true` and `message_id`.
- Owner decision 2026-10-19 (plan default was docx-preview + SheetJS): SheetJS's npm build is stale with known CVEs and its CDN is unreachable from the build, so the readers are the product's own, like its xlsx writer.

## What are the limits?
- A part may unpack to 64 MB and a package to 256 MB (measured with `statIndex()` before anything is read); past that it is refused with "too large to show here". A sheet shows 2000 rows and 200 columns, a workbook 120000 cells; a document 600000 characters of HTML, pictures of 2 MB each and 8 MB together (the rest become "[picture]"); `truncated` and `images_lost` say so and the viewer prints a note. Not read: .doc/.xls (binary), .pptx, charts, shapes, headers and footers, comments, styles beyond the ones above, merged cells (a merged area shows its first cell), formulas without a cached value (empty, marked ƒx).
- XML is loaded with `LIBXML_NONET` and never `LIBXML_NOENT`. `pg_office_zip_stream()` restores the built-in `zip://` wrapper and libxml's loader once, because PHPExcel (the .xls/.ods importers) replaces both for the rest of a request and XMLReader cannot open a part through them; the suite hit this ordering.
- `get_file.php` gives docx, docm, dotx, xlsx, xlsm, xltx and pptx their MIME type without `attachment` (the browser decides; a desktop one saves them); unlisted forms (dotm, xltm, pptm, …) go as octet-stream attachments.
- A part read as a stream (shared strings, sheet rows) stops at the size the package declares for it, and the shared string table at 500,000 strings or 32 MB: `zip-entry-declared-size.md`.
- A part built into a DOM may declare 8 MB; a longer document is read from its beginning, rows and strings node by node: `office-dom-ceiling.md`.
