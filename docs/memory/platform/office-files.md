## How is a .docx or .xlsx shown?
- Read on the server by `includes/fn/office.php` (PR #530) and drawn by `assets/js/pg_office_view.js`, fetched on first use. No library: a worksheet is walked with XMLReader a row at a time (the way `import_products_f.php` reads one), a document with DOMDocument. A workbook comes back as sheets of rows of strings (`pg_office_xlsx_read()`: every sheet in the workbook's order, hidden ones flagged, shared and inline strings, booleans, dates by the cell's style, numbers without exponent, days counted from 1904 where the workbook says so, the sheet's own row numbers so gaps stay, and per row `calc`: the columns holding a formula with no stored result, which the viewer marks ƒx); a document as a small safe subset of HTML (`pg_office_docx_read()`: headings by style id, paragraphs with bold/italic/underline/strike/super/sub, alignment, bullet and numbered lists from the paragraph or its style, tables with column spans, http(s)/mailto links, pictures inlined as data: URLs, tabs, page breaks; deleted tracked text left out). Everything the file wrote is escaped; nothing passes through as markup.
- Doors: the file manager's `explorer_office_preview` (file window and Quick Look, `check_view_access()` on the folder; an ERP document to whoever may read the ERP) and the Workspace's `ws_file_office` (the preview window, a Preview button on the file card and in the Files tab; channel readers). The file payload carries `office: true` and `message_id`.
- Owner decision 2026-10-19 (plan default was docx-preview + SheetJS): the npm build of SheetJS is stale and carries known CVEs and its own CDN is not reachable from the build, so the readers are the product's own, like its xlsx writer.

## What are the limits?
- A part may unpack to 64 MB and a package to 256 MB (measured with `statIndex()` before anything is read); past that the file is refused with "too large to show here". A sheet shows 2000 rows and 200 columns, a workbook 120000 cells; a document 600000 characters of HTML, pictures of 2 MB each and 8 MB together (the rest become "[picture]"); `truncated` and `images_lost` say so and the viewer prints a note. Not read: .doc/.xls (binary), .pptx, charts, shapes, headers and footers, comments, styles beyond the ones above, merged cells (a merged area shows its first cell), formulas without a cached value (empty, marked ƒx).
- XML is loaded with `LIBXML_NONET` and never `LIBXML_NOENT`. `pg_office_zip_stream()` restores the built-in `zip://` wrapper and libxml's loader once, because PHPExcel (the .xls/.ods importers) replaces both for the rest of a request and XMLReader cannot open a part through them; the test suite hit this ordering.
- `get_file.php` now names the OOXML MIME types (docx, xlsx, pptx, their macro and template forms); they still go out as attachments.
