Local changes (re-apply after every update):
1. css/bootstrap.css and css/bootstrap.min.css, the check/radio/switch row
   rule of .form-field (grid-column: 2). Upstream selector:
     .form-field:has(> .check, > .radio, > .switch) > :not(.check, .radio, .switch)
   Replaced with (same elements, same specificity 0,3,0):
     .form-field > :not(.check, .radio, .switch):is(:has(~ :is(.check, .radio, .switch)), :is(.check, .radio, .switch) ~ *)
   In bootstrap.min.css the same text without spaces:
     .form-field>:not(.check,.radio,.switch):is(:has(~:is(.check,.radio,.switch)),:is(.check,.radio,.switch)~*)
   Reason: while a sheet holds a rule that styles every child of a :has()
   element, Chromium restyles the whole document on every element added or
   removed; on the visual editor that made each click take about a second.
   The .map files are left as published. tests/css_has_restyle_test.php
   fails when an update brings the upstream form back.
