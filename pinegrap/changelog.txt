  R E L E A S E   N O T E S
  ----------------------------------------------------------

  What each update brings to your site.
  The newest version is at the top.

    [FIX]        bug fixes
    [SECURITY]   security fixes

2026.11.0
[NEW] Added a code editor to the visual designer: the page is edited as HTML beside the canvas, and the design's CSS and JS files open in the same editor, with find and replace.
[NEW] Added editing of a record's fields (product, stock, order, contact, current account, product group, offer, event) from the Workspace record drawer, and the /price and /stock commands in channels, with the change kept in the channel as a decision.
[NEW] Added export and import of Workspace notes: one note as Markdown or a printable page, all notes as a zip that opens as a vault in other note apps, and Markdown or text files, or a zip of them such as an Obsidian vault or a Notion, Joplin or Bear export, brought in as notes.
[NEW] Added operations alerts: a failed or stale backup, a stopped scheduled task, changed software files, a burst of PHP errors, low disk space, failing e-mail or webhooks and an expiring certificate are told on the panel bell, by e-mail and on subscribed devices.
[NEW] Added a backup before every software update and repair: the files it replaces and the database are saved first, and Repair Software can roll the files back to it.
[NEW] Added a maintenance mode that shows visitors a maintenance page while staff see the site, and a health address for uptime monitors.
[NEW] Added the pre-information form and the distance sales contract to checkout: the customer accepts both before ordering, and the copy they accepted is kept with the order.
[NEW] Added Word and Excel previews in the file manager and the Workspace, where the cells of an Excel file and the paragraphs of a Word file can also be edited, and the file manager's files in Workspace channels: a file is sent to a channel from the file manager, or picked from the writing box.
[NEW] Added Write with AI: Pinegrap AI, or the browser's built-in AI on a site without it, drafts a product's descriptions, the browser title and description of products and pages and the text of form records from what the form holds, and a draft goes into the field only when you use it.
[NEW] Added responsive images to the visual designer: each picture is sent at the width the screen needs, as AVIF or WebP where the browser reads it, and pictures below the first load as the visitor scrolls to them.
[NEW] Added product feeds for shopping platforms and AI shopping assistants: the catalog is published every day as an OpenAI product feed (JSON Lines or CSV) and a Google Merchant Center file, each at its own private address.
[NEW] Added revision history to the visual designer: every publish of a page is kept with who published it and when, and a revision can be compared with the page as it is now and loaded back into the editor; a page can also be given a time to go on the site and a time to come off it.
[NEW] Added a Docker setup in the docker/ folder: the site, its web server, the general job and the database start with one docker compose command, and the first start can install the site without the installation wizard.
[NEW] Added automatic invoices for web orders: the ERP issues an order's invoice on its own once the order is paid or once it is shipped, as the store chooses, and the panel bell names any order it could not invoice.
[NEW] Added redirects to short links: a short link can send visitors on with a 301 or 302, its name can be an old address with Turkish letters, a * in the name covers the addresses under it that no page answers, links can be imported and exported as a CSV file, a renamed page keeps its old address, and a 404 log lists the addresses visitors did not find, each with a button that starts a redirect for it.
[NEW] Added AI crawler rules and llms.txt: robots.txt can say how AI tools may use the site and ask the crawlers you do not allow to stay away, in step with the firewall, and the site can publish llms.txt, a short guide to its pages for AI tools.
[NEW] Added a personal data export for KVKK and GDPR requests: everything the site keeps about one person downloads as a JSON file and a PDF summary, and every export is recorded in the audit trail.
[NEW] Added Trendyol to marketplace sync: categories and their attributes, listings opened from product groups, stock and prices sent on a schedule, orders imported and accepted, and Trendyol's order webhook.
[NEW] Added customer returns: a customer asks to return items from their account within the withdrawal period, staff approve or reject the request with a note and the return carrier and code, follow the goods back, refund the money through the payment provider or record a refund made another way, and are reminded before a refund falls due.
[NEW] Added verified-buyer product reviews: only a customer who bought a product can review it, the review is marked as a verified purchase, and the star rating of verified reviews is given to search engines.
[NEW] Added Stripe card payments on the PaymentIntents API: the card, Apple Pay and Google Pay are entered in Stripe's own fields on the checkout page, with 3-D Secure, and refunds and cancellations go through Stripe from the order screen.
[NEW] Added address filters to the visual designer's record lists: one page lists the records of an author, a tag or a section named in its address, with its own title, heading, search engine address and RSS feed for each value.
[NEW] Added the News Portal design template: a front page, section pages and writers, author submissions that editors review and schedule, most read, a breaking strip, and the account, newsroom and e-mail pages.
[NEW] Added the Long-Read Magazine design template: a long-read layout with a cover story, issues, sections and contributor pages, author submissions that editors review and schedule, and the account, editors' desk and e-mail pages.
[NEW] Added passkeys: with two-step verification on, a person adds a passkey (fingerprint, face or screen lock, or a security key) on their account page and uses it in place of the authenticator code when signing in.
[NEW] Added Cloudflare Turnstile and hCaptcha as optional CAPTCHA providers beside the built-in question: the answer is checked with the provider when a form is sent, and a form is refused while the provider cannot check it.
[NEW] Added double opt-in for forms that add to a contact group: a per-form switch mails a signed confirmation link, the sender joins the group only after pressing the button behind it, and the request and the confirmation are kept in the consent log.
[NEW] Added a built-in page cache: pages that look the same for every visitor who is not signed in are kept on the server and sent from there, with standard cache headers for a CDN or reverse proxy in front.
[NEW] Added a Community Forum & Docs design template: boards and topics with replies, pinned topics and announcements, guides that members write and moderators review, reported posts, a moderation dashboard, and the account and e-mail pages.
[NEW] Added ready-made connectors to the Workspace's MCP servers: DeepWiki, Linear, Notion, GitHub and others are added with one click, and any other server from its address alone, the way it signs in found by checking it.
[NEW] Added a Comments widget to the visual designer: a page's comments and the form that adds one are drawn in the design's own layout, while each page keeps its own comments and comment settings.
[FIX] Miscellaneous bug fixes and new features.
[SECURITY] Implemented various security fixes.
