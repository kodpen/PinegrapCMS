5. Right of withdrawal

The buyer may withdraw from the contract within fourteen days without giving any reason. The period starts on the day the goods reach the buyer or a person the buyer named; for services it starts on the day the contract is made. To withdraw, the buyer informs the seller in writing at the address or e-mail address above before the period ends.

The buyer sends the goods back within ten days of the notice. The seller refunds every payment received, delivery costs included, within fourteen days of receiving the notice, using the means of payment the buyer used.

The right of withdrawal does not apply to goods made to the buyer's specifications or clearly personalised, goods that spoil quickly, sealed hygiene goods opened after delivery, and digital content or services supplied immediately with the buyer's consent.

6. Complaints

Complaints can be sent to the seller at the contact details above, and disputes can be taken to the consumer bodies or courts competent where the buyer lives.
