4. Delivery

If the order cannot be fulfilled, the seller tells the buyer and refunds every payment received within fourteen days.

5. Right of withdrawal

The buyer may withdraw from this contract within fourteen days of receiving the goods without giving any reason and without penalty. For services the period starts on the day the contract is made. The buyer sends the goods back within ten days of the notice; the seller refunds every payment received, delivery costs included, within fourteen days of receiving the notice.

6. Disputes

Disputes arising from this contract are settled by the consumer bodies and courts competent where the buyer lives.
